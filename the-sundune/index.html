The Sundune: Palm Springs Vacation Rental with 3 King Beds

Palm Springs, California

Coastal, unhurried, ten minutes from Palm Canyon.

Built for three couples who all want a real bed.

The Sundune is a two-bedroom condo in central Palm Springs, and the thing that makes it unusual is simple: three king beds, two full bathrooms, and the Uptown Design District close enough to walk to. Early light comes through the linen curtains, and the copper fixtures and aloe-toned walls do their work quietly. It doesn't announce itself.

The design pulls from surf and sand without being a caricature of either, sun-bleached textures and natural materials and the kind of place that makes you actually want to stay in. Three king beds means three couples can share without anyone drawing straws over the pull-out, which almost never happens in Palm Springs rentals.

Why Palm Springs, Why This One

I wanted a Palm Springs property with a real downtown address, actual king beds across every bedroom, and a design that felt specific to somewhere. Most places give you one king and two queens, which means somebody always ends up with the short straw. The Sundune doesn't do that. Everyone gets a proper bed and a door that closes.

The mornings here are honestly the best part. Early light through linen curtains, coffee made, nowhere you have to be yet. I extended the first stay I did there just to have more of them.

Location

The Uptown Design District is the part you can walk, so the vintage stores, galleries, and coffee are all on foot. Downtown Palm Springs and Palm Canyon Drive are about a ten-minute drive, The Surf Club roughly five minutes by car, Indian Wells Tennis Garden twenty-five, Coachella and Stagecoach at Empire Polo Club about thirty, and Joshua Tree forty-five minutes when you're ready to leave civilization behind for a bit.

Who Stays Here

Usually three couples who've already done the math on king beds, or a group that cares where they wake up in the morning and wants a place that was put together with some thought. Also a lot of people who've been searching for three real king beds in Palm Springs long enough to know that this kind of layout is harder to find than it should be.

Frequently Asked Questions

The questions I get most often, answered honestly.

What is the design vibe at The Sundune? +

Coastal-desert. Sun-bleached textures, natural materials, copper fixtures and aloe-toned walls. Early-morning light through linen curtains, the pace of a long weekend that has nowhere to be. Not trendy, not overdone. The kind of place that feels like it's been there a while, even though we just finished it.

Is The Sundune walkable to downtown Palm Springs? +

Not downtown, no. Downtown Palm Springs and Palm Canyon Drive are about a ten-minute drive. The Uptown Design District is the walkable piece, so galleries, vintage stores, and coffee are all on foot from here.

How close is The Sundune to downtown Palm Springs? +

About ten minutes by car to downtown and Palm Canyon Drive. The Uptown Design District is closer and easy on foot, which covers most of a lazy afternoon.

How many guests does The Sundune sleep? +

Up to 6 guests across 2 bedrooms, with 3 king beds total, a full kitchen, private balcony, and the shared HOA pool at Palm Canyon Villas.

Are pets allowed at The Sundune? +

Yes. One pet is welcome with a $150 pet fee. Contact us at indigopalmco@gmail.com before booking to confirm your situation.

What is the minimum stay at The Sundune? +

4 nights minimum, per the Palm Canyon Villas HOA requirement. Check-in at 4pm, checkout at 11am. Early check-in or late checkout sometimes works if the schedule is open, just ask ahead of time.

How much do I save booking directly? +

Airbnb charges guests a 20% service fee on the subtotal, which adds up fast on a five-night minimum. Booking direct cuts that out entirely, same house, no platform fee on top.
