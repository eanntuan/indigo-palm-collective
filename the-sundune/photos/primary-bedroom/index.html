THE SUNDUNE

Primary Bedroom

Two kings, a surfboard, and a desk with a pool view.

Blue leaf wallpaper sits behind two rattan headboards, with a vintage wood longboard mounted between them. The surfboard looks intentional because it is. An arched wood mirror catches the morning light, and the banana leaf plant in the corner is one of the few that stays alive indoors without supervision.

The desk is the part guests mention in reviews. A 58-inch standing desk sits in front of the window with the pool and palm trees right outside, and a rattan armchair and round marble side table are tucked into the corner for when you want to be off the clock but not in bed. Rust linen curtains soften the light.

Two king beds side by side, good linen, a chunky knit throw on each. The bathroom with the green herringbone shower opens straight off the room. Three kings at The Sundune, and two of them are here, so this is the room that books first for couples traveling together.
