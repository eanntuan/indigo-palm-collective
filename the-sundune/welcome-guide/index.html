At the Door

Entering the Condo
  • 1

    Door Code

    Your code comes through at least 24 hours before check-in. It's active from 4 pm on arrival day until 11 am checkout.

  • 2

    Black Screen Door

    Open the black screen door first, it's unlocked. The blue door behind it has the Schlage keypad.

  • 3

    Blue Schlage Keypad

    Enter your 4-digit code. If the keypad is dark, press the bottom left corner to wake it. Don't tap anywhere else first, Schlage counts any touch as a keystroke. If it resists, pull the handle toward you to align the lock, then try again.

  • 4

    Inside

    Lights are on the left as you walk in. The pool key hangs on the coat rack by the door. Don't lose it, $300 to replace and I will definitely know.

Things to Do

Get Out

Palm Springs Aerial Tram

6,000 ft up to San Jacinto Peak in about 10 minutes, and it goes from desert heat to alpine cold in that same ride. Get there early on weekends, the line gets long.

pstramway.com ↗

Moorten Botanical Garden

Cactarium full of rare cacti and succulents you genuinely won't find anywhere else. Give it an hour, and check the gift shop before you leave, they have plants worth dragging home.

moortenbotanicalgarden.com ↗

Modernism Week

11 days in February covering architecture tours, home tours, film, and design panels. Book the specific events you want early, a lot of them sell out weeks out.

modernismweek.com ↗

Palm Springs Art Museum

Desert art, European works, and a solid Native American collection. Free on Thursdays from 4 to 8pm, which is worth knowing.

psmuseum.org ↗

Coachella + Stagecoach

Two weekends each in April: Coachella for pop, electronic, and indie, Stagecoach for country. Both completely take over the valley and both are 20 minutes from here.

coachella.com ↗ stagecoachfestival.com ↗

The V Pool Party

Weekend pool parties at The Sonder Hotel with DJs and the classic Palm Springs day-drinking vibe. No ticket, just show up.

thevpalmsprings.com ↗

Palm Springs Vintage Market

MCM furniture, clothing, and art on the first Sunday of the month, October through May. If you want the good stuff, be there by 8am.

palmspringsvintagemarkets.com ↗

El Paseo, Palm Desert

Open-air shopping strip with a decent mix of high-end brands and independent boutiques, mountain views the whole way down. 20 minutes from here, good for a half-day.

elpaseo.com ↗
