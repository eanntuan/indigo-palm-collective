Primary Bedroom

King bed. Pink accent wall. Green velvet sitting area. En-suite.

The primary bedroom leads with a pink/mauve accent wall and a green velvet tufted headboard. Macrame hammock wall art above the bed. Mustard throw blanket at the foot. Two matching nightstands with gold lamps and fiddle leaf figs in the corner.

The sitting area doubles as a reading nook: two green velvet chairs with gold legs, a navy graphic wallpaper detail on the closet wall behind, and a mustard knit pouf. The guestbook lives on the nightstand. The en-suite bathroom is visible from the bed through the open doorway.

This is the room the adults fight over. The kids' rooms are equally thought out, but this one has the dedicated sitting area and the en-suite. Worth calling dibs early.
