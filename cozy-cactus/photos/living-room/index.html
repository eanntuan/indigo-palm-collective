LIVING SPACES

Living Room

Open concept. Foosball, dining, game room all in one flow.

The living room runs open into the dining area, with the game room just off to the side. Gray linen sofas face the built-in TV niche, kilim rug underfoot. The ceiling fan keeps it comfortable on the afternoons when the desert earns its reputation.

Dining table seats six with tan leather chairs and a round gold mirror overhead. The foosball table gets serious use. Abstract diptych art above it, fiddle leaf in the corner, entry bench with the kilim tile top by the front door.

Everything is labeled. The snack drawer has snacks. Nothing here requires you to ask where anything is.
