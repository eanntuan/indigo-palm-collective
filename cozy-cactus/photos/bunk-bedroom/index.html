BUNK BEDROOM

Bunk Bedroom

Queen + bunk beds. Chevron wall. Sleeps 4.

The chevron wall runs floor to ceiling in coral, teal, and salmon pink. Black metal bunk beds on one side of the room, a queen bed on the other. The bunk has a book ledge. A desk sits along the wall with a framed Palm Springs illustrated map above it and white lamp for reading.

Sound machine in this room. Kids figure out the bunk hierarchy fast. Parents appreciate the queen option for a teenager or a third adult who'd rather not fight over the mural bedroom.

This is the room that makes multi-family trips work. Four people, one room, no one stuck on an air mattress.
