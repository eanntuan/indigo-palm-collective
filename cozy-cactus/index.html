The house where the whole family actually exhales.

"It's the first time we have truly rested during our vacation with our 3 children." That mom rebooked before checkout. You'll see the same thing said differently in review after review across 146 of them.

Pack-n-play is in the family amenities closet, ready when you need it. Sound machines in the kids' rooms. The labeled drawer system guests mention constantly is exactly what it sounds like: snack drawer has snacks, junk drawer has no junk. Stokke high chair at the kitchen counter, coffee bar stocked and ready at 6am when the toddler decides that's morning now.

Three bedrooms, two bathrooms, sleeps eight. Turquoise and coral run through every room, and the kids' bunk room has a toy collection with real range: art supplies, books, outdoor gear. The community pool is steps away for afternoons when the backyard isn't enough, and the private hot tub gets genuinely good after 9pm, once the kids are finally down.

Why I Built It This Way

I got tired of staying places that called themselves family-friendly and meant they had a pack-n-play somewhere in a closet, probably unfolded wrong. So I built what I actually wanted to stay in: every drawer labeled, every kid thing anticipated, every logistical headache handled before you arrive.

The moment I keep thinking about is 9pm, kids finally down, and you're in the hot tub watching the sky go dark over the mountains. Nothing left to figure out. That's the one I kept building toward.

Eann

Location

Cozy Cactus is inside Indian Palms Country Club, a gated community in Indio with walkable pickleball, tennis, golf, and a second community pool on site.

Empire Polo Club is walking distance from Indian Palms, straight up Eisenhower Drive on foot, no shuttle required. Indian Wells Tennis Garden is twenty minutes east for BNP Paribas, Palm Springs thirty minutes west, and Joshua Tree forty-five minutes northeast. Downtown Indio is five minutes in any direction.

Who Stays Here

Mostly families with young kids who want a rental actually built for them, not just technically child-tolerant, plus multi-family groups splitting bedrooms and sharing the backyard, and festival-goers who need a real home base over a party house. If you notice details, you'll find them everywhere here.

Frequently Asked Questions

The stuff people ask before they book.

How far is Cozy Cactus from Coachella and Stagecoach? +

Walking distance to the Empire Polo Club, out through the gate and up Eisenhower Drive on foot. During festival weekends, community members within Indian Palms run golf cart rides.

Is Cozy Cactus good for families with young children? +

It's built specifically for families with young kids. Pack-n-play, sound machines, Stokke high chair, a real toy collection, art supplies, pool safety gate, and the labeled drawer system guests mention constantly. One parent called it "the first vacation where I rested" and rebooked before checkout.

Does Cozy Cactus have a pool and hot tub? +

No private pool here, but there is a private hot tub in the backyard plus three heated community pools at Indian Palms, the closest one steps from the backyard gate. The outdoor space has lounge chairs, outdoor dining, a fire pit, and a 2-hole putting green.

Are pets allowed at Cozy Cactus? +

No pets at Cozy Cactus. If you're traveling with dogs, Terra Luz in Indio takes up to 2 dogs with a $150 pet fee. Email indigopalmco@gmail.com to confirm before booking.

What is the minimum stay at Cozy Cactus? +

2-night minimum. Check-in at 4pm, checkout at 10am. Early check-in or late checkout sometimes works depending on the schedule, so just ask ahead of time.

How much do I save booking directly? +

Roughly 20% compared to booking on Airbnb or VRBO, since Airbnb adds a 20% guest service fee to your subtotal and we skip all of that when you book direct.
