Why Indio

The honest case for staying in Indio

Most people search "Palm Springs vacation rental" by default. Understandable. But if you're going to Coachella, Stagecoach, or Indian Wells, Indio is actually better. Here's why.

You're closer to the festival

Empire Polo Club is 2.5 miles from both of our houses, about 8 minutes by car and 19 minutes on foot via Eisenhower Drive. Palm Springs is 30 minutes the other direction. Every morning you leave Palm Springs for the polo grounds is 30 minutes you didn't sleep.

The price difference is real

Indio runs 25-35% less per night than comparable Palm Springs rentals, especially during festival season. Same desert sun, same mountain views, dramatically fewer guests competing for the same square footage.

Indian Palms is a proper neighborhood

Both houses are inside Indian Palms Country Club: gated community, walkable pickleball and tennis, a second community pool on site. It's quiet in the way that matters. No noise ordinance complaints at 2am, just actual neighbors.

More house per dollar

3 bedrooms, private pools, real outdoor space. The kind of square footage that in Palm Springs would cost you a third more at minimum. Groups of 6-8 especially feel this. Nobody is sleeping on a fold-out couch.
