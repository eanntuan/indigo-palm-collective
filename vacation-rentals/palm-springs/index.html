Why Palm Springs

The honest case for staying in Palm Springs

Palm Springs isn't just a backdrop. It's a city with a real walkable downtown, a design legacy that holds up, and mountain views that justify the drive out from LA. Here's why it works.

Mid-century in its natural habitat

Palm Springs invented this aesthetic. Staying here means it's out the window, not just a Pinterest board. The architecture is real, the history is intact, and the neighborhood context actually matches what you booked.

Walk to Palm Canyon Drive

Dinner, cocktails, ice cream after the aerial tram. The Sundune is 10 minutes from the main drag by car, walkable on the right evening. The restaurant scene is legitimately good, and the bar crawl doesn't require a designated driver if you're feeling ambitious.

20 minutes from Indian Wells

BNP Paribas Open in March, Indian Wells Open year-round. If tennis is the trip, Palm Springs makes the drive comfortable: easy freeway, no real traffic outside peak festival weekends, and you sleep somewhere worth coming back to.

Pool access, no hotel hours

The Sundune has access to the community pool at Palm Canyon Villas, a smaller HOA pool shared with the neighborhood, not a hotel pool with wristbands and 5pm closing. That distinction matters more than it sounds after a long day at the tram or a late dinner on Palm Canyon.

FAQ

What people ask before booking

What is the minimum stay at The Sundune in Palm Springs? +

The Sundune is in a managed community that sets a 4-night minimum on weekday stays and a 5-night minimum for stays that include a weekend night. This is an HOA requirement, not our preference. It affects availability during short festival weekends. Email us directly at indigopalmco@gmail.com if you're trying to fit a shorter window.

Is The Sundune pet-friendly? +

Dogs are welcome with prior approval. Email indigopalmco@gmail.com before booking with your dog's details (size, breed, number). The community pool at Palm Canyon Villas is steps away, which makes it manageable for dogs staying home during day trips to the tram or Joshua Tree.

How far is The Sundune from the BNP Paribas Open? +

About 20 minutes from Indian Wells Tennis Garden on highway 111. March is one of the best months in the desert for weather, and the BNP Paribas draws top-10 players from both tours. Palm Springs is where most BNP visitors stay, and The Sundune puts you in the center of it.

Is Palm Springs better than Indio for Coachella? +

Depends on the trip. Palm Springs gives you a real city to wander between festival days. Indio puts you 8 minutes from the polo grounds and saves you 30-45 minutes each way in festival traffic. If you're primarily going for Coachella, our Indio properties are the stronger call. If Palm Springs is part of the trip and the festival is secondary, The Sundune works.
