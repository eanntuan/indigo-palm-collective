This agreement is between MPT Industries (Owner) and the booking guest (Guest) for the selected Indigo Palm Collective property.

1. Check-In and Check-Out

Check-in is at 4:00 PM. Check-out is at 10:00 AM. Late check-out requires prior approval and may incur a fee.

2. Maximum Occupancy

The property may not exceed the maximum guest count listed at booking. Unauthorized extra guests are grounds for immediate termination without refund.

3. No Smoking

Smoking is strictly prohibited inside the property and within 25 feet of any entrance. This includes cigarettes, cigars, vapes, and cannabis. Violation results in a minimum $250 fee.

4. No Parties or Events

No parties, gatherings beyond the booked guest count, weddings, or commercial filming are permitted. Violations result in immediate removal without refund.

5. Quiet Hours

Quiet hours are 10:00 PM to 8:00 AM. Outdoor noise, amplified music, and pool use must cease by 10:00 PM.

6. Pet Policy

Dogs are welcome at The Sundune and Terra Luz with prior written approval. Pets are not permitted at The Cozy Cactus under any circumstances. An unauthorized pet fee of $150 per night applies if this rule is violated.

7. Damage Responsibility

Guest is responsible for any damage beyond normal wear and tear. Damage will be documented and billed to the card on file. Guest agrees to report any pre-existing damage within 2 hours of check-in.

8. Pools, Hot Tubs, and Amenities

No glass near the pool or hot tub. No pets in the pool or hot tub. Use of all amenities is at Guest's own risk. Misuse may result in cleaning fees.

9. Cancellation Policy

Cancellation terms are as stated at the time of booking. Cancellations outside the eligible window are non-refundable. Travel insurance is strongly recommended.

10. No Refunds for Events Beyond Owner's Control

No refunds for weather, power outages, pool or appliance downtime, construction noise, or other events outside Owner's control.

11. Entry

Owner or authorized agents may enter the property for maintenance, repairs, or emergencies with reasonable notice when possible.

12. Liability

Guest assumes all risk associated with use of the property and holds Owner harmless from liability except in cases of Owner's gross negligence.

13. Governing Law

This agreement is governed by California law. Disputes are resolved in Riverside County, California.
