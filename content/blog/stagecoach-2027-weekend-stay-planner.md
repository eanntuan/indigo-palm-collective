<p><a href="https://www.stagecoachfestival.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><strong>Stagecoach</strong></a> 2027 is scheduled for <strong>April 23-25</strong> at the Empire Polo Club in Indio, the weekend after Coachella Weekend 2 wraps up. It is the same grounds as Coachella with a different crowd, a different pace, and a different set of things to plan for.</p>

<p>This post is the planning checklist for a group using Indio as its base. If you are still weighing camping, hotels, and rentals, our <a href="/blog/stagecoach-2027-where-to-stay/">Stagecoach where-to-stay guide</a> covers that comparison. If you are leaning toward a house, keep reading.</p>

<p>Dates are as announced by the festival at the time of writing. Passes, lineup, and policies can change, so check the <a href="https://www.stagecoachfestival.com/faqs" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">official festival info</a> before you finalize plans. We have left the lineup out on purpose so this page does not go out of date.</p>

<h2>Before you book: what matters for a Stagecoach group</h2>

<p><strong>Real beds for everyone.</strong> Count bedrooms and bed sizes, not just the "sleeps" number. Three long days outdoors are easier when everyone has an actual bed.</p>

<p><strong>Walking distance, confirmed.</strong> Indian Palms Country Club in Indio is walking distance to the Empire Polo Club. <a href="/terra-luz/">Terra Luz</a> and <a href="/cozy-cactus/">The Cozy Cactus</a> are both inside it, and both sleep eight. For any other rental, ask how you would get to the grounds and how long it takes.</p>

<p><strong>Somewhere to cool down.</strong> A private pool, a hot tub, shade, and a fridge full of cold drinks all help. Terra Luz has a private saltwater pool and a heated spa. The Cozy Cactus has a private hot tub and shared community pools nearby.</p>

<p><strong>The full total.</strong> Compare the whole cost of the stay, not only the nightly rate. There is a section on this near the end.</p>

<h2>Thursday: arrive and stock the kitchen</h2>

<p>Plan to arrive on Thursday, before the festival starts. Do a single grocery run for the weekend (here is <a href="/blog/grocery-stores-coachella-valley/">our guide to grocery stores in the valley</a>), start the first night with something easy, and get to bed at a reasonable hour. If you are bringing boots, wear them around the house for an hour or two first. New boots are not a Friday-morning decision.</p>

<h2>Friday to Sunday: pacing the days</h2>

<p><strong>Be strategic about entry.</strong> The festival's FAQ notes that crowds peak on Friday and Saturday from 4pm to 7pm, with the longest waits, and says to arrive early to allow for traffic and the security search at the entrance. If you are walking in, timing your arrival away from that window helps. Decide as a group which sets matter most and plan your entry around those instead of around opening time.</p>

<p><strong>Know the bag rules before you pack.</strong> According to the festival's FAQ, a medium backpack must fit within 18 inches tall, 13 wide, and 8 thick when packed, and purses or handbags are allowed up to 12 by 6 by 12 inches. The FAQ also lists restrictions on items like professional cameras, wagons, and large umbrellas, and shows allowed and not-allowed chair examples. Read the full list on the festival's FAQ page before the group packs.</p>

<p><strong>Use the house in the middle of the day.</strong> If your base is walking distance, you can go back for a shower, a meal, and a rest before the evening. That is one of the main reasons to stay close. The round trip is short enough to be worth it.</p>

<p><strong>Pick up and drop off where the festival says to.</strong> For friends and family who are not walking, the FAQ lists a pick-up and drop-off area near Avenue 52 and Madison Street. Exact locations are shown on the pre-event maps, which the festival posts a few days before the event.</p>

<h2>Sunday night and Monday</h2>

<p>Pack on Sunday before you head out for the last night. Know your checkout time, and decide before you leave who will do a last sweep of the house. If you have a long drive home on Monday, leave some buffer in your morning.</p>
