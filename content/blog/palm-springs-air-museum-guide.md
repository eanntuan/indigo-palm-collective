<p>The Palm Springs Air Museum is at 745 N Gene Autry Trail, open every day from 10 am to 5 pm, with adult tickets at $26 and kids 12 and under free with a paid adult. Plan on two to three hours inside air-conditioned hangars holding more than 80 aircraft, many of them still flyable.</p>

<p>That makes it one of the better indoor things to do in Palm Springs when the afternoon hits 110 degrees, and a solid fallback on the rare rainy day. Hours and prices change, so check the museum's own <a href="https://palmspringsairmuseum.org/visit/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">visit page</a> before you go.</p>

<h2>Palm Springs Air Museum Tickets and Hours</h2>

<p>The <a href="https://palmspringsairmuseum.org/" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>Palm Springs Air Museum</strong></a> opens daily at 10 am and closes at 5 pm. It closes on Thanksgiving, Christmas, and the first Saturday in February for its gala, which falls on February 6 in 2027.</p>

<p>General admission is $26 for adults 18 to 64. Seniors 65 and up, teens 13 to 17, and veterans or retired military pay $24, and kids 12 and under come in free with a paying adult. Family packs run $68 for two adults and one teen, up to $99 for two adults and three teens, and active-duty military and their immediate families are free. A $5 Museums For All rate covers up to four adults with an EBT card and government ID.</p>

<p>Buy tickets on the museum's <a href="https://palmspringsairmuseum.org/admission/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">admission page</a> or call 760-778-6262. Prices come from that page today and can change, so confirm before you go.</p>

<h2>What to See at the Palm Springs Air Museum</h2>

<p>The museum shows more than 80 aircraft across about 91,000 square feet of hangar space, and a good share of them are still in flyable condition. The collection runs from World War II through Korea, Vietnam, and the Global War on Terror. The hangars have no ropes, so you can stand right under a wing.</p>

<p>The museum's site names a P-40 Warhawk, an F-104 Starfighter, a C-47, a T-28, a Blue Angels F/A-18 Super Hornet, and Walt Disney's Gulfstream I among the highlights. Smaller jets from later wars sit both inside and out on the ramp.</p>

<p>Between the planes, the museum schedules guest speakers, mostly Saturdays at 1 pm from October through May, free with admission. The visit page lists a cafe, so check there for its hours or pack water and a snack.</p>

<h2>Warbird Rides and Cockpit Sits</h2>

<p>Two experiences go beyond looking. On Saturdays from 10 am to noon, the museum opens a vintage aircraft so you can sit inside while a docent walks you through its history, at $5 per adult. Which plane is open changes, so confirm on the museum's site.</p>

<p>The bigger ticket is a warbird ride. The museum flies restored WWII aircraft over the Coachella Valley every weekend, with rides starting at $250. Book those through the Warbird Rides page or by calling 760-482-1838, and ask what the ride includes before you commit. Gift certificates exist, so it works as a present for the aviation fan in your group.</p>

<h2>How Long to Spend at the Air Museum</h2>

<p>Two to three hours covers the hangars at an easy pace, reading the placards as you go. A quick lap runs closer to an hour, and aviation fans can burn most of a day. A warbird ride adds time on top, so book it for the end of your visit.</p>

<p>The museum is also a reasonable stop with kids, since children 12 and under are free and the exhibits are meant to be seen up close. Our <a href="/blog/palm-springs-with-kids/">Palm Springs with kids guide</a> shows where it fits in a family itinerary. Friendly dogs on a leash are welcome, and all hangars are reachable by wheelchair, walker, or scooter, with a few loaner wheelchairs available first come, first served.</p>

<h2>Palm Springs Air Museum FAQ</h2>

<h3>How much are Palm Springs Air Museum tickets?</h3>
<p>Adults 18 to 64 pay $26, seniors 65 and up, teens 13 to 17, and veterans or retired military pay $24, and kids 12 and under are free with a paid adult. Family packs start at $68 for two adults and one teen. Confirm current prices on the <a href="https://palmspringsairmuseum.org/admission/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">admission page</a>.</p>

<h3>How long does it take to see the Palm Springs Air Museum?</h3>
<p>Plan on two to three hours if you read the placards. A quick walk through takes about an hour, and aviation fans can spend most of a day. A warbird ride adds time on top.</p>

<h3>Can you sit in the planes at the Palm Springs Air Museum?</h3>
<p>On Saturdays from 10 am to noon the museum opens a vintage aircraft for cockpit sits at $5 per adult, with a docent explaining the history. Confirm which plane is open on the museum's site before you drive over.</p>

<h3>Is the Palm Springs Air Museum good for a hot or rainy day?</h3>
<p>Yes. The hangars are air-conditioned and the main exhibits are indoors. Some aircraft are displayed outside, so in summer go at opening and keep the outdoor part short.</p>

<h3>How do I get Palm Springs Air Museum tickets?</h3>
<p>Buy them online through the Admission page at palmspringsairmuseum.org, or call 760-778-6262. Warbird rides are booked separately at 760-482-1838.</p>
