<p>Halloween lands on a Saturday this year, which in the Coachella Valley means one thing: the weekend of Friday, October 30 and Saturday, October 31 is going to be loud, costumed, and booked up. I started pulling this list together because guests keep asking me what is happening, and the answer is spread across six different city websites.</p>

<p>I used <a href="https://www.palmspringslife.com/the-guide/things-to-do/9-spooktacular-things-to-do-for-halloween-2026-in-greater-palm-springs/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Palm Springs Life's roundup</a> as a starting point, then checked every event against the organizer's own page. One of their nine didn't survive that check and another only partly did (details near the bottom). Everything below is confirmed as of October 8, 2026, but dates move, so click through before you buy anything.</p>

<div class="quick-summary">
<p><strong>Quick version:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Costumed dogs: Cathedral City dog contest Sat Oct 24, free</li>
<li>Kids and families: Cathedral City Spooktacular Sun Oct 25, free; Living Desert Howl-O-Ween Sat Oct 31</li>
<li>Big night out: Halloween Palm Springs on Arenas Road, Oct 30 and 31, 4 to 10 PM</li>
<li>Seated and spooky: Dracula comedy at Revolution Stage, Candlelight concert in Rancho Mirage, Graveyard Shift dinner</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2>The week before Halloween</h2>

<h3>Howl-O-Ween Dog Costume Contest, Cathedral City</h3>
<p>Saturday, October 24, 9 to 11 AM at the dog park inside <strong>Panorama Park</strong> (28905 Avenida Maravilla). Sign-up opens at 9 and judging starts at 9:30. It's free, with first through third place in both a small-breed and a large-breed division, plus a "Best Combo Costume" prize for a dog and its person. Cathedral City's Parks and Community Events Commission runs it, and Cathedral City Grocery Outlet sponsors.</p>

<p>This is the one I'd bring a dog to first. Small, local, nobody is taking it too seriously.</p>

<img src="/blog/images/palm-springs-mcm-neighborhood.webp" alt="Mid-century modern home with desert landscaping in a Palm Springs neighborhood on a clear fall day" width="1600" height="1067" loading="lazy">
<p class="image-caption">October in the valley is shorts-and-costume weather, usually. Check the forecast anyway.</p>

<h3>Cathedral City Halloween Spooktacular</h3>
<p>Sunday, October 25, noon to 3 PM at <strong>Civic Center Plaza</strong> in front of City Hall (68700 Avenida Lalo Guerrero). This is the 10th annual, with free admission, kids' trick-or-treating, a costume contest, bicycles to win, and vendors handing out candy. It's a daytime event built for families with small kids, so go for the sugar, not the scares.</p>

<h3>Dracula: A Comedy of Terrors, Palm Springs</h3>
<p><a href="https://www.revolutionstagecompany.com/events/dracula-a-comedy-of-terrors-2026-10-29-19-00" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>Revolution Stage Company</strong></a> (611 S Palm Canyon Dr, Suite 16) opens its season with a 90-minute stage farce where a female Van Helsing chases a very vain Count. Performances are at 7 PM on October 15, 16, 22, 23, 29, 30, and 31, with a 3 PM matinee on Sunday, October 25. It's a good pick if you want Halloween energy without a bar crawl, and the theater sits right on Palm Canyon, so dinner beforehand is easy.</p>

<h2>Halloween weekend, Friday and Saturday</h2>

<h3>Halloween Palm Springs, Arenas District</h3>
<p>This is the big one. The <a href="https://www.halloweenpalmsprings.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>Arenas District</strong></a> in downtown Palm Springs runs a street party both nights, October 30 and 31, from 4 to 10 PM, with DJ sets on the main stage. Friday is free with a suggested $10 donation that goes to LGBTQ charities, Saturday is free, and VIP tickets are sold separately on both nights.</p>

<p>The costume contest is Saturday only, with more than $2,000 in prizes across Grand, first, and second place. Registration runs 6:00 to 7:15 PM and the organizers say 7:15 is a hard stop, so get in line early. You also need to be at the stage by 7:30.</p>

<img src="/blog/images/ps-orange-door-mcm.webp" alt="Bright orange mid-century front door on a Palm Springs home" width="1800" height="1800" loading="lazy">
<p class="image-caption">Palm Springs doesn't need much help looking theatrical.</p>

<p>If you're staying at <a href="/the-sundune/">The Sundune</a>, plan on driving or taking a rideshare: downtown is about 10 minutes away by car, not a walk. That's a feature on Halloween night, because nobody wants to find parking near Arenas at 9 PM.</p>

<h3>Candlelight: Halloween Classics, Rancho Mirage</h3>
<p>Friday, October 30 at 8:30 PM, inside <strong>The Ritz-Carlton, Rancho Mirage</strong>. It's a candlelit string quartet playing Halloween material, and tickets are sold through <a href="https://feverup.com/en/palm-springs-ca-us/candlelight" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Fever</a>. Fever's listing calls it "Halloween Classics," while Palm Springs Life uses a longer title and names a specific quartet, so I can't tell you who is playing. The date, venue, and time match across sources, and that's what I'm confident in.</p>

<h3>Graveyard Shift at PS Underground</h3>
<p>Saturday, October 31, 7 to 9:30 PM at <a href="https://www.psunderground.com/upcoming-experiences" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>PS Underground</strong></a> in Palm Springs. The ticket is $199 and covers a secret four-course dinner, live entertainment, a little dancing with the Gand Band, and an open bar of beer, wine, specialty cocktails, and mocktails. It's a splurge, and it's the only seated dinner on this list, so I'd book it early.</p>

<img src="/blog/images/terra-luz-patio-evening-lights.webp" alt="Terra Luz patio in Indio lit with string lights at dusk" width="1200" height="1800" loading="lazy">
<p class="image-caption">The Terra Luz patio at dusk, my preferred way to end a Halloween night.</p>

<h3>Howl-O-Ween at The Living Desert, Palm Desert</h3>
<p>Saturday, October 31, 9 AM to 1 PM at <a href="https://www.livingdesert.org/events/upcoming-zoo-events/" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>The Living Desert Zoo and Gardens</strong></a> (47900 Portola Ave). The zoo lists it as included with regular admission, with trick-or-treat stations, festive activities, and animal encounters. Bring a reusable bag for the candy.</p>

<p>Dog owners, one thing I can confirm from the zoo's calendar: it lists a "Dog Day at the Zoo" on Friday, October 30, where you add a dog ticket to your own. Palm Springs Life describes a howl-themed costume photo day with different hours, and I couldn't match those details, so check the zoo's page for the current hours and dog pricing before you go.</p>

<h2>Where I'd stay for Halloween weekend</h2>

<p>I'm biased, obviously. But the geography matters: <a href="/terra-luz/">Terra Luz</a> in Indio is dog-friendly (with approval) and has the private pool and patio for a low-key night after the crowds. The Sundune in Palm Springs is dog-friendly too and puts you roughly 10 minutes from the Arenas party and Revolution Stage. The Cozy Cactus doesn't take pets, so if a costumed dog is part of your plan, it's one of the other two.</p>

<p>I don't have a verified Indio-specific Halloween event to point you to, so from Indio you're driving about half an hour to Palm Springs for most of this. If you're traveling with a dog, my <a href="/blog/pet-friendly-palm-springs/">dog-friendly Palm Springs guide</a> covers what to skip. And if you want the full picture of the Indio side, see the <a href="/blog/terra-luz-dog-friendly-coachella/">Terra Luz dog-friendly guide</a>.</p>

<h2>What I dropped, and why</h2>

<p>Palm Springs Life also listed a Spooktacular Themed Skate at Berger Foundation Iceplex in Palm Desert on October 30. The Iceplex's own events page shows a Spooktacular Skate tied to October 16 instead, and I couldn't confirm the October 30 date, so I left it out. If you want to skate, call them at 760-835-5000.</p>

<p>The Living Desert's costumed-dog day only half-matched, which is why that section sticks to what the zoo's own calendar says.</p>

<h2>Frequently Asked Questions</h2>

<h3>What is happening in Palm Springs on Halloween night 2026?</h3>
<p>Halloween Palm Springs takes over the Arenas District from 4 to 10 PM on Saturday, October 31, with DJs and a costume contest that has more than $2,000 in prizes. Graveyard Shift at PS Underground runs 7 to 9:30 PM for $199, and Dracula: A Comedy of Terrors plays at Revolution Stage at 7 PM.</p>

<h3>Are there Halloween events for kids in the Coachella Valley?</h3>
<p>Yes. The Cathedral City Spooktacular on Sunday, October 25 is free and runs noon to 3 PM with trick-or-treating and a costume contest. Howl-O-Ween at The Living Desert on Saturday, October 31 runs 9 AM to 1 PM and is included with zoo admission.</p>

<h3>Is there a dog Halloween costume contest in Palm Springs?</h3>
<p>The closest is the Howl-O-Ween dog contest in Cathedral City on Saturday, October 24, 9 to 11 AM at Panorama Park. It's free, with separate small and large breed prizes and a best dog-and-owner combo award.</p>

<h3>Is Halloween Palm Springs on Arenas Road free?</h3>
<p>General entry is free on both nights, October 30 and 31, with a suggested $10 donation on Friday for LGBTQ charities. VIP tickets cost extra and are sold through the event's website.</p>

<h3>Can I walk to the Arenas District from The Sundune?</h3>
<p>No. Downtown Palm Springs is about a 10-minute drive from The Sundune, so plan on a car or rideshare. Only the Uptown Design District is walkable from the property.</p>

<h3>Which Indigo Palm rentals are dog-friendly for Halloween weekend?</h3>
<p>Terra Luz in Indio and The Sundune in Palm Springs both accept dogs with prior approval. The Cozy Cactus does not. Mention your dog when you inquire at <a href="/terra-luz/">indigopalm.co/terra-luz</a> or <a href="/the-sundune/">indigopalm.co/the-sundune</a>.</p>
