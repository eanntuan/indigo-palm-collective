<p>The best wine bar in Palm Springs for most people is <a href="https://www.canopypalmsprings.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>Canopy Wine Lounge</strong></a> on N Palm Canyon Drive, with <strong>Counter Reformation</strong> at the Parker for a wine-first night. Both want a reservation on weekends.</p>

<p>Palm Springs has plenty of cocktail bars, but dedicated wine bars are fewer, smaller and tend to keep short hours. That makes them a good palm springs date night, as long as you plan around the days they close. Cocktails live in the <a href="/blog/palm-springs-bars/">best bars in Palm Springs guide</a>, and this one sticks to wine.</p>

<p>Every address, hour and booking detail below comes from the venue's own site. Hours shift with the season here, so confirm before you go.</p>

<h3 id="canopy">Canopy Wine Lounge</h3>

<p><strong>Canopy Wine Lounge</strong> is at 175 N Palm Canyon Drive, which makes it the simplest anchor for a downtown evening. The list is built around producers who care about sustainability, craftsmanship and a distinct sense of place, and the menu pairs it with light bites, filling plates and sweet treats.</p>

<p>Hours are Sunday and Monday 3 to 8:30pm, Thursday 3 to 8:30pm and Friday and Saturday 3 to 9:30pm, closed Tuesday and Wednesday. Happy hour runs daily from 3 to 5pm, which suits an early start before dinner somewhere else. Reservations go through <a href="https://www.opentable.com/r/canopy-wine-lounge-palm-springs" target="_blank" rel="noopener">OpenTable</a>, and the room also runs a wine membership called The Cru.</p>

<h3 id="ash-vine">Ash &amp; Vine</h3>

<p><a href="https://www.ashandvinepalmsprings.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>Ash &amp; Vine</strong></a> is a restaurant first, so treat it as the wine-with-dinner stop. It sits at 19 La Plaza and serves brunch 9am to 1pm, bar bites from 3 to 5pm on the bar side only, and dinner 5 to 9pm, with reservations advised through OpenTable.</p>

<p>Outdoor dining is dog-friendly, which matters in a town where half the couples travel with one. The restaurant's site also lists an Ash &amp; Vine Bar across the way at 20 La Plaza, so check the site for its current status before you plan around it.</p>

<h3 id="jalama">Jalama Wines</h3>

<p>Jalama Wines keeps a tasting room in the same La Plaza courtyard. The winery makes Pinot Noir and Syrah from Lompoc, so a pour here is a producer tasting rather than a bar list. Check <a href="https://www.jalamawines.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">jalamawines.com</a> for current tasting hours and bookings, because tasting rooms rarely keep bar hours.</p>

<h3 id="v-wine-lounge">V Wine Lounge &amp; Martini Bar</h3>

<p><a href="https://www.vwinelounge.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>V Wine Lounge &amp; Martini Bar</strong></a> is at 600 E Tahquitz Canyon Way, on the east side of downtown. The site describes a midcentury-style lounge with a scenic outdoor patio, and it keeps separate main and martini menus, so a group where half the table wants wine and half wants a martini can split the difference.</p>

<p>Reservations are by phone at (760) 668-9665. Check the contact page on the venue's site for current hours, since closing times vary by weekday.</p>

<h3 id="counter-reformation">Counter Reformation</h3>

<p><a href="https://www.parkerpalmsprings.com/counter-reformation" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>Counter Reformation</strong></a> sits inside the Parker at 4200 E Palm Canyon Drive, and the Parker describes it as a Parisian tabac, a Cave à Manger with a rotating by-the-glass list built around variety and discovery. Food comes as small savory plates, listed on a menu called Fishes &amp; Loaves, with wines on a separate Libations menu.</p>

<p>It opens Wednesday through Sunday from 5 to 11pm and is closed Monday and Tuesday. Bookings open 30 days ahead through the Parker's reservation page, where you pick a date, time and seat. Standing at the bar and leaning on ledges is the format, so it suits two people far better than a group of eight.</p>

<p>It is a car or rideshare trip from downtown, and the Parker's grounds are worth arriving a little early for.</p>

<h2>Wine Bars Near Uptown and The Sundune</h2>

<p>If you are staying at <a href="/the-sundune/">The Sundune</a>, plan on a short drive or rideshare to downtown, about ten minutes. For something closer to the Uptown Design District, Sottovoce is an Italian coffee and wine bar at 1345 N Palm Canyon Drive inside The Shops at Thirteen Forty-Five. Check <a href="https://www.sottovocecafe.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">its site</a> for hours before heading over.</p>

<h2 id="palm-desert">Wine Bars in Palm Desert and the Rest of the Valley</h2>

<p>The valley's other dedicated wine bar is <a href="https://lafewinebar.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><strong>La Fe Wine Bar</strong></a> at 73900 El Paseo in Palm Desert. It has been locally owned since 2017 and expanded in 2021, with a tapas-style menu of charcuterie boards, salads, flatbreads, empanadas and desserts, plus wine by the glass or bottle, cocktails and craft beer.</p>

<img src="/blog/images/wine-bars-palm-springs-1.webp" alt="Charcuterie board with cured meats and cheeses, the kind of shared plate served at wine bars" width="1600" height="1037" loading="lazy">

<p>Happy hour runs daily 5 to 6pm, and the site advertises a charcuterie board with a select bottle of wine for $50, with the wine changing regularly. Tasting and pairing events need a reservation because space is limited, and there is a Wine Room for buying bottles plus a wine club. Call (760) 565-1430 to confirm hours, since the site does not list them. Dinner nearby is covered in the <a href="/blog/best-restaurants-palm-desert/">Palm Desert restaurant guide</a>.</p>

<p>Indio is the gap. The dedicated wine bars are in Palm Springs and Palm Desert, so if you are staying near the polo grounds, treat El Paseo as a drive and check a map for the current time. <h2>Wine Bars in Palm Springs: Frequently Asked Questions</h2>

<h3>What is the best wine bar in Palm Springs?</h3>
<p>Canopy Wine Lounge at 175 N Palm Canyon Drive is the easiest all-around pick, with a producer-focused list, a food menu and a downtown address. Counter Reformation at the Parker suits a wine-first evening if you can book ahead.</p>

<h3>Do Palm Springs wine bars take reservations?</h3>
<p>Canopy and Ash &amp; Vine book through OpenTable, Counter Reformation opens bookings 30 days out through the Parker, and V Wine Lounge takes reservations by phone. Weekends fill first.</p>

<h3>Which Palm Springs wine bars are closed midweek?</h3>
<p>Canopy is closed Tuesday and Wednesday, and Counter Reformation is closed Monday and Tuesday. Check each venue's own site before planning a midweek night.</p>

<h3>Is there a good wine bar near Indio?</h3>
<p>The dedicated wine bars sit in Palm Springs and Palm Desert. La Fe Wine Bar on El Paseo is the one worth planning a drive around from Indio.</p>

<h3>What is a good Palm Springs date night plan with wine?</h3>
<p>Early pour at Canopy or Ash &amp; Vine, a downtown walk, then a later seat at Counter Reformation if you both like a serious list. Confirm hours first, since several wine bars close early or midweek.</p>
