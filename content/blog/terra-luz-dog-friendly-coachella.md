<h2>What Terra Luz Offers Dogs</h2>

<p>The thing that matters most is the yard. Terra Luz has a fenced backyard and a grassy side yard, which sounds basic until you've stayed somewhere that advertised a "yard" and handed you a patio with a decorative fence a beagle could clear. Here the gate latches and the fence is the real kind, so a dog can sniff around, nap in the shade, and burn off energy without a leash or a babysitter at the slider.</p>

<p>The grassy side yard is the part dogs care about. Most of the Coachella Valley is gravel, decomposed granite, and pavers, which is hard on paws and hard on a dog who just wants to find a soft spot. Real grass on a hot afternoon is a small thing that turns out to matter a lot.</p>

<p>The outdoor kitchen is built for the way people use this house, which is to say outside, most of the day, with the dog underfoot. You can grill dinner while your dog patrols the yard, and nobody has to choose between being social and watching the gate.</p>
