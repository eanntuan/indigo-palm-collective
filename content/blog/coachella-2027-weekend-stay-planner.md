<p><a href="https://www.coachella.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"><strong>Coachella</strong></a> 2027 is scheduled for two weekends at the Empire Polo Club in Indio: <strong>April 9-11</strong> (Weekend 1) and <strong>April 16-18</strong> (Weekend 2). The lineup is the same both weekends, so the choice usually comes down to your calendar and what you can find for a place to stay.</p>

<p>This post is about the part that gets less attention than the lineup: how the weekend actually runs when your base is in Indio. If you are still deciding between Indio and Palm Springs, start with <a href="/blog/coachella-2027-where-to-stay/">our where-to-stay guide</a>. If you have already decided on Indio, this is the planning checklist.</p>

<p>Dates are as announced by the festival at the time of writing. Passes, lineup, and policies can change, so treat <a href="https://www.coachella.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">coachella.com</a> as the final word.</p>

<h2>Before you book: five things to check</h2>

<p><strong>The real sleeping setup.</strong> "Sleeps 8" can mean four proper beds and four makeshift ones. Count the bedrooms and bed sizes, then count the people. Three full days of festival walking make a real bed more valuable than any amenity.</p>

<p><strong>How close it really is.</strong> A listing that says "near Coachella" can mean anything from a short walk to a long drive in festival traffic. Indian Palms Country Club in Indio is walking distance to the Empire Polo Club, and both <a href="/terra-luz/">Terra Luz</a> and <a href="/cozy-cactus/">The Cozy Cactus</a> are inside it. Ask any host what "near" means in minutes and in how you would get there.</p>

<p><strong>A place to be at 3pm.</strong> Between the afternoon sets and the headliners, a lot of groups want a break from the sun. A pool, shade, and a kitchen make that break easy. Terra Luz has a private saltwater pool and a heated spa. The Cozy Cactus has a private hot tub and shared community pools nearby.</p>

<p><strong>A kitchen that can handle breakfast.</strong> Festival food costs what festival food costs. A kitchen that can feed a group coffee and eggs before you leave saves money and gets everyone out the door on time.</p>

<p><strong>The full total, not the nightly rate.</strong> Cleaning fees, service fees, and taxes change the number. See the compare section near the end of this post.</p>

<h2>Thursday: arrive, shop, keep it easy</h2>

<p>The festival runs Friday through Sunday, so Thursday is your buffer. Aim to arrive in daylight, drop the bags, and do one grocery run for the whole weekend. We keep a <a href="/blog/grocery-stores-coachella-valley/">grocery guide for the valley</a> if you want to know where to go.</p>

<p>Then keep the first night simple. A pool, an easy dinner, and an early night will do more for your Saturday than any dinner reservation. Save the restaurant plans for a night when you are not about to spend three days on your feet. If you want ideas, <a href="/blog/best-restaurants-near-coachella/">here is where we eat near the festival</a>.</p>

<h2>Friday to Sunday: a simple daily rhythm</h2>

<p>Every group has its own pace, but the shape of a good festival day is usually the same:</p>

<p><strong>Slow morning.</strong> Coffee, a real breakfast, sunscreen on before you leave. Fill water bottles. Decide as a group what time you will leave and who is carrying what.</p>

<p><strong>Head over once the sets you care about get close.</strong> You do not need to be at the gates at opening unless there is a specific act you want. Walking from a base in Indio means you can come and go on your own schedule.</p>

<p><strong>Break in the afternoon.</strong> If the sun is hitting hard and nobody you love is playing, go back. Cool down, eat something that is not fried, nap if you want. This is the main reason a base near the grounds matters: the round trip has to be short enough that it is worth it.</p>

<p><strong>Back for the evening.</strong> You will be fresher for the headliner than the people who stayed in the sun for eight hours.</p>

<p><strong>One person's job: the exit plan.</strong> Decide before the night starts how you are getting home and what you will do if the group splits up.</p>

<h2>Getting in and out</h2>

<p>The festival publishes its transportation details on its <a href="https://www.coachella.com/getting-here" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Getting Here page</a>. As of the festival's most recent published information (the 2026 edition), it lists four pedestrian entrances, each marked by a colored path, with bikes allowed at the same four intersections and motorized scooters prohibited. It also lists a rideshare area on Avenue 49 at Monroe Street with the longest waits expected between midnight and 2:30am, and notes that day parking is not guaranteed.</p>

<p>Those details were published for 2026, and the festival updates them every year, so confirm the 2027 version before you finalize plans. The practical takeaway does not change much: if you can walk home, the late-night rideshare line is not your problem.</p>

<h2>Sunday night and Monday</h2>

<p>Do the packing before the last night. A Sunday-night house that is half packed is easier to leave on Monday morning than a house you have to sort through on three hours of sleep. Check the checkout time ahead of time and let the group know the plan before you head out for the final night.</p>
