Best Mexican Restaurants Palm Springs & Coachella Valley

By Eann Tuan · October 6, 2026 · 8 min read

The best Mexican restaurants in Palm Springs are Tac/Quila, a Michelin-listed taqueria, and Las Casuelas Terraza, a family patio institution. For Mexican food in Indio, start with One Stop Taco Shop and Arriola's Tortilleria, then head to Coachella for El Tranvia and to Palm Desert and Rancho Mirage for sit-down dinners.

The valley's deepest tortilla knowledge sits about 30 miles east of Palm Springs, in Indio and Coachella, where farm-town taquerias came long before the festivals. This guide runs city by city, east to west, and every address and hour comes from the restaurant's own site or a reputable listing (where listings disagree, I say so). For the rest of the valley's food, start with the Coachella Valley food guide.

Best Mexican Food in Indio

Indio is where I'd send you first, and it rarely shows up on a "best of Palm Springs" list. The two spots below are takeout-style places where you eat out of paper and nobody cares about the plating.

One Stop Taco Shop

One Stop Taco Shop sits at 84051 Indio Blvd, near Van Buren, and it is listed as a gas station business, which tells you how much energy goes into the décor. The menu runs asada, al pastor and cabeza tacos on handmade corn or flour tortillas, plus quesadillas, burritos, flautas, sopes, and aguas frescas like horchata and jamaica. Delivery-app prices put tacos around $5 each, though in-shop prices may differ.

One Stop Taco Shop on Indio Blvd, in the middle of the Coachella Valley's taco belt.

Check hours before you go, since online listings disagree, and the shop's phone is (760) 342-0874. Our Indio local gems guide covers the rest of the neighborhood.

Arriola's Tortilleria

Arriola's Tortilleria at 82721 Wilson Ave is a family-run tortilleria that has been around for decades, and everything is made to go, with no dining room. Burritos, tamales, nachos, and masa for your own tamales are the core of it, and the burritos come filled with shredded beef in red sauce, shredded pork in green, or both. Reviews mention waits of 20 to 30 minutes, so call ahead at (760) 347-7782.

Arriola's Tortilleria on Wilson Ave is a takeout counter, so plan to eat at the house.

Listed hours vary by source, so confirm by phone before a special trip.

Indio International Tamale Festival

If you are here in early December, the 34th annual Indio International Tamale Festival runs Saturday and Sunday, December 5 and 6, 2026, with free admission, a tamale-eating contest, a recipe competition, and a parade. The City of Indio confirms the dates, but sources differ on the exact venue, so check the festival site before you go.

The Indio Tamale Festival, held the first weekend of December.

Staying in Indio puts all of this close. Terra Luz and The Cozy Cactus are both walking distance to the Empire Polo Club, with outdoor kitchens and private yards for the takeout you'll inevitably bring home. Check availability →

Mexican Food in Coachella

El Tranvia

El Tranvia is at 1221 6th St in Coachella, a short drive from Indio, with dine-in, takeout and delivery. The menu leans on birria, including a charbroiled birria tatemada and a quesabirria platter, and reviewers also praise the shrimp and cheese gobernador tacos. Reviewers disagree on whether the birria is goat or beef, so ask when you order if it matters to you.

El Tranvia on 6th St in Coachella.

Listed hours range from early morning to 8pm on weekdays and to midnight on weekends depending on the source, so call (760) 391-9772. Reviews also disagree on whether it serves beer, which is another reason to ask first.

Best Mexican Restaurants in Palm Desert and Rancho Mirage

The middle of the valley is where Mexican food gets dressed up a bit, and it suits a sit-down dinner. For more of El Paseo, see our Palm Desert restaurant guide.

Mole Mexican Kitchen

Mole Mexican Kitchen is at 73-130 El Paseo, Suite M, in The Shops on El Paseo, and the sauce is the premise. The menu includes a mole con pollo and a Colorado lamb shank in ancho chile mole, and Yelp regulars name the shrimp enchiladas, churros and flan. Reservations are available at (760) 636-1269, and the restaurant's site has current hours, which have shifted recently.

A Palm Desert restaurant patio near El Paseo, for the feel of the area.

Las Casuelas Nuevas

Las Casuelas Nuevas opened in Rancho Mirage in April 1973 at 70-050 Highway 111, built by the Delgado family as a Spanish colonial hacienda with materials imported from Mexico. The tree-covered patio, designed in 1984 and modeled on the stonework of San Miguel de Allende, runs under twinkle lights, and the restaurant says its recipes trace back to the founder's grandmother in Mazatlan. Current hours are Monday through Thursday 11am to 8pm, Friday and Saturday 11am to 9pm, and Sunday 10:30am to 8pm. Call (760) 328-8844.

Best Mexican Restaurants in Palm Springs

Palm Springs has two answers to the question, and they could hardly differ more. One is a Michelin-listed taqueria with a tight menu, the other a family institution with a sprawling patio and live music. For the whole town, our Palm Springs restaurant guide goes wider.

Tac/Quila

Tac/Quila is at 415 N Palm Canyon Dr and appears in the Michelin Guide, which describes the tortillas as made in house and the portions as generous. Michelin specifically recommends the pork tamale and the quesabirria short rib tacos served with consommé, and for dessert the tres leches cake or the churro ice cream sandwich. The menu also covers shrimp ceviche, mango habanero aguachile, vegan jackfruit enchiladas, and a tequila and mezcal list from small distilleries.

Carnitas, carne asada and al pastor, the three fillings on most valley taqueria menus, shown here from an Oregon food truck. Photo: Larry Miller / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 2.0.

Michelin lists hours as 11am to 10pm daily, with indoor and patio seating, and reservations go through the restaurant at (760) 417-4471.

Las Casuelas Terraza

Las Casuelas Terraza is at 222 S Palm Canyon Dr, run by the same Delgado family that opened the original Las Casuelas in downtown Palm Springs in 1958. The restaurant has three patio styles: a quiet Terraza with an antique fountain, a livelier Palapa with an outdoor bar, dance floor and bandstand, and a sidewalk Cantina. The tequila list tops 100 bottles, the margaritas are hand-shaken, and live music plays on weekday evenings and all day on weekends.

South Palm Canyon Drive in downtown Palm Springs, the same strip as Las Casuelas Terraza. Photo: cultivar413 / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY 2.0.

Check hours on their site, call (760) 325-2794 for tables, and if you are staying at The Sundune, this is an easy first-night dinner in town.

How to Plan a Mexican Food Day in the Coachella Valley

A workable order is tacos or burritos in Indio at lunch, then a sit-down dinner in Palm Desert, Rancho Mirage or Palm Springs. Early December changes the plan, because the tamale festival becomes the meal. If you can't decide which end of the valley suits you, Palm Springs versus Indio lays out the tradeoffs.

This is a starting list. La Quinta, Cathedral City and Desert Hot Springs each have more taquerias worth finding.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best Mexican restaurant in Palm Springs?

Tac/Quila on North Palm Canyon is the pick with outside validation, since it is listed in the Michelin Guide and makes its tortillas in house. Las Casuelas Terraza on South Palm Canyon is the old-school answer, run by the same family that opened the original Las Casuelas in 1958. Go to Tac/Quila for tacos and to Terraza for a patio and a margarita.

Where can I find good Mexican food in Indio?

Try One Stop Taco Shop on Indio Blvd for tacos and Arriola's Tortilleria on Wilson Ave for burritos, tamales and fresh tortillas to go. In early December the Indio International Tamale Festival fills downtown with tamale vendors. Check each shop's hours before you drive over, because online listings disagree.

Is there good Mexican food near the Coachella and Stagecoach grounds?

Yes. One Stop Taco Shop and Arriola's are both in Indio, and El Tranvia is in Coachella, a short drive from Indio. Terra Luz and The Cozy Cactus sit within walking distance of the Empire Polo Club, so you can eat in town and head back to the house.

What is the oldest Mexican restaurant in the Coachella Valley?

The Delgado family opened the original Las Casuelas in Palm Springs in 1958, and Las Casuelas Nuevas in Rancho Mirage followed in 1973. No source I found names the oldest in the valley, but both are among the longest-running family Mexican restaurants here.

Do I need reservations for Mexican restaurants in Palm Springs?

Tac/Quila and Las Casuelas Terraza both take reservations, and a Friday or Saturday dinner or a festival weekend is when you want one. The taquerias in Indio and Coachella are counter service and walk-in.
