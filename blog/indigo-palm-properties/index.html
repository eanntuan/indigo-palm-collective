4 Indigo Palm Vacation Rentals: Which One Is Yours?

By Eann Tuan · July 2, 2026 · 7 min read

People ask me all the time which property they should book. My honest answer: it depends on what your group wants when you're not performing vacation.

Indigo Palm Collective has three short-term rental properties in the Coachella Valley, each one designed with a very specific kind of guest in mind. They are genuinely different from each other, not just aesthetically, but structurally. Who they sleep, what they're near, what the vibe feels like at 9pm when everyone's tired. If you read through this, you'll know which one is yours before you even check availability.

Terra Luz: Indio, CA

For groups who want something that feels like a place, not a rental.

Terra Luz is a 3-bedroom, 2-bath house in Indio that started with a blue kitchen backsplash and grew into a full Latin/Cuban aesthetic from there. Talavera tile. Terracotta floors. A kitchen with a marble waterfall island and an open layout that makes cooking feel like part of the trip. A Kahlo blue pool in the backyard surrounded by an outdoor kitchen, pergola, and string lights. The wild tropics bedroom has a canopy bed and ceiling art that guests photograph every single time.

This is the property for groups who care about the interior as much as the pool. The people who send photos of the kitchen before they even unpack. It sleeps up to 8 comfortably across three bedrooms: the primary suite, the Wild Tropics room, and the Cali Room (a king bed with a built trundle and a hidden workstation behind bifold doors, Kahlo blue of course).

Terra Luz is in Indio, about 25 minutes from Palm Springs, and walking distance to the Empire Polo Club (Coachella and Stagecoach festival grounds).

Book Terra Luz direct at indigopalm.co/terra-luz: no platform fees.

The Cozy Cactus: Indio, CA

For families who need it to work, not just look good in photos.

The Cozy Cactus was the first Indigo Palm property, bought Easter Sunday 2022 after a Facebook Marketplace furniture chase through Culver City and Indio. It's a 3-bedroom house inside Indian Palms Country Club, which means a gated community, HOA manicured grounds, and access to golf, putting green, and the clubhouse.

The design direction is mid-century desert meets "parents rest here." Every drawer is labeled. There's a Stokke high chair, a proper Pack 'n Play, a dedicated game room with foosball, board games, and a setup built for groups that include a six-year-old and a teenager in the same trip. There are three shared community pools in the complex and a private hot tub out back, the yard is enclosed, and the layout keeps kids contained while adults stay comfortable.

If someone in your group has been on a vacation rental trip where the baby had nowhere to sleep and the kitchen had two plates and no wine opener, this is the antidote. Also walking distance to the Empire Polo Club: the Coachella and Stagecoach festival grounds.

Book The Cozy Cactus direct at indigopalm.co/cozy-cactus: no platform fees.

The Sundune: Palm Springs, CA

For couples or small groups who want Palm Springs proper, not the desert suburbs.

The Sundune is a 2-bedroom, 2-bath apartment in Palm Springs, not a house, intentionally. The design leans coastal-desert: sun-faded surf culture meets Palm Springs retro. Distressed denim blues, warm sand tones, a balcony that catches the morning light before the heat arrives. It's the quietest property in the portfolio, the one that doesn't try to entertain you indoors: it trusts Palm Springs to do that.

This is the property for smaller groups: couples, or two friends who want the Uptown Design District within walking distance and a space that feels personal. The apartment sits in East Palm Springs near Waverly Drive, a short walk to Uptown, a quick drive to the downtown strip, close to hiking trailheads, and far enough from the loud blocks that you can sleep.

No private pool (there's a community pool), offset by the fact that you're in Palm Springs, with Uptown walkable and downtown a short drive rather than the longer haul you'd make from Terra Luz or Cozy Cactus.

Book The Sundune direct at indigopalm.co/the-sundune: no platform fees.

The Well: Palm Springs, CA

The Well is the fourth Indigo Palm property, a 1BR/1BA in Palm Springs with a sage-and-dusty-blue interior and a longer, slower energy than the short-term rentals. Available for extended stays. If you're interested, reach out through indigopalm.co.

How to choose

A quick version:

  • Group of 6–8, wants a wow moment when you walk in: Terra Luz
  • Family with kids under 10, needs it to be functional: The Cozy Cactus
  • 2–4 people, want Palm Springs proper and walkability: The Sundune
  • Long-stay, slow pace, extended desert time: The Well
