Palm Springs Christmas 2026: Parade, Tamales and Lights

By Eann Tuan · October 14, 2026 · 8 min read

By the first week of December the sun is down by about 5 PM, the valley cools into jacket weather, and every strand of string lights in the Coachella Valley starts earning its keep. This is the Christmas version of the desert, and it comes with a parade, a tamale festival, a drive-through light show on the same grounds as Coachella, and a tram ride up to real snow.

I own rentals in both Indio and Palm Springs, so I pulled the dates into one place. Everything with a date below was checked against the organizer or venue page on October 14, 2026. A few of the big light displays haven't published their 2026 schedules yet, and I say so where that's the case instead of guessing.

Quick version:

  • Fri Dec 4: Palm Springs tree lighting and carols, 5:15 PM, free
  • Sat Dec 5: Festival of Lights Parade, 5:45 PM, free, and day one of the Indio Tamale Festival (Dec 5 and 6, free)
  • Sun Dec 6: Aerial Tramway tree lighting, 5:30 PM
  • All season: Magic of Lights in Indio, WildLights in Palm Desert, Candy Cane Lane in Cathedral City
  • Snow: only at the top of the Tram, never on the valley floor

The first weekend of December does the heavy lifting

December 5 is the first Saturday of the month, which is when Palm Springs holds its parade, and this year it's also the first day of the tamale festival in Indio. That puts two free events about 25 miles apart on the same Saturday, roughly 30 to 40 minutes by car on the I-10 (my Indio vs. Palm Springs guide has the full comparison).

Friday, December 4: Palm Springs Tree Lighting and Carols

The city's holiday tree lighting is listed for 5:15 PM, free, and Visit Palm Springs describes caroling, children's crafts, a visit from Santa, and free cookies and hot cocoa. In 2025 it took place at Frances Stevens Park (500 N. Palm Canyon Drive), and I couldn't find a 2026 location announcement, so confirm the venue before you drive over.

Saturday, December 5: Festival of Lights Parade

The 34th annual Festival of Lights Parade starts at 5:45 PM on Palm Canyon Drive in downtown Palm Springs, free, under the theme "Desert Dreaming: A Retro Holiday." Expect marching bands, floats, giant holiday balloons, and Santa, with a live broadcast on NBC Palm Springs. The City of Palm Springs posted the details in its parade announcement.

Sources disagree on where the route ends: Visit Palm Springs says Tachevah Road, while Marching.com says Tamarisk Road. I'd treat "Palm Canyon north of Ramon Road" as the safe summary and PSFestivalOfLights.com as the tiebreaker. Since Palm Canyon is the route, I'd park a few blocks off it and walk in rather than circle the corridor at 5:30.

Saturday and Sunday, December 5 and 6: Indio International Tamale Festival

The 34th Indio International Tamale Festival runs both days in downtown Indio, and the City of Indio lists admission as free. It bills itself as the world's largest tamale festival, and I can't verify the superlative but I'm prepared to believe it once you see the vendor lines. If you want the full picture of the town around it, my Indio local gems guide and things to do in Indio cover the rest of Old Town.

The pinata-style MERCADO arch is the landmark, with lowriders parked on the grass underneath it.

Hours depend on who you ask: the festival's Facebook page says noon to 8 PM, while another listing says 10 AM to 7 PM, and listings place it at Miles Avenue Park. Check the festival site the week of. If you want tamales and the parade on the same day, eat in Indio around midday and point the car west by about 3:30 to give yourself parking time before the 5:45 start.

Sunday, December 6: Aerial Tramway Tree Lighting

The Tram's own tree lighting is listed for 5:30 PM, with a 45-foot tree and more than 4,000 lights according to Visit Palm Springs. I couldn't confirm from the Tram's site which station hosts it, so check pstramway.com before you plan the evening around it.

If you're picking where to sleep for this weekend, decide that first and the events second. Indio puts you next to the tamales and the Magic of Lights grounds, Palm Springs puts you next to the parade, and the full breakdown of my three rentals is near the bottom of this post.

Lights you can see all season

None of these had published a 2026 schedule where I could find it on October 14, so the dates below are last year's pattern, labeled as such. Check each organizer's page before you buy anything.

Magic of Lights, Indio

This is a drive-through show at the Empire Polo Club (81-800 51st Avenue), priced per carload instead of per person. The club's public events page lists it for November and December 2026, and last year it ran November 18 to December 31, opening at 5:30 PM and closing at 9 PM Sunday through Thursday and 10 PM Friday and Saturday. Last year's regular price was $35.99 per vehicle, and tickets are sold through the Magic of Lights locations page.

These are the same grounds Coachella and Stagecoach use, which are walking distance from Indian Palms Country Club, though a drive-through means the car comes along regardless.

WildLights, Palm Desert

The Living Desert Zoo and Gardens (47900 Portola Ave) turns the zoo into a ticketed light walk on select nights. In 2025 it ran from November 25 to December 30, 6 to 9 PM, closed on Christmas Day, with advance reservations recommended because capacity is limited each night. The ticket page showed no 2026 dates yet, so watch the Living Desert site for the WildLights schedule. If you're coming with little ones, my Coachella Valley with kids guide has the rest of the family-friendly list.

Candy Cane Lane, Cathedral City

It's a residential street, Minerva Road between Tachevah Drive and Avenida La Vista, and it's free. There are no official hours, the lights come on at dark, and weekends and the days closer to Christmas run busiest. Go earlier in the evening, since neighbors live there, and park and walk if you can. Palm Springs Life profiled some of the families behind it.

One more date worth a pencil mark: The Shops on El Paseo in Palm Desert held its tree lighting on the Saturday before Thanksgiving in 2024 and 2025 (November 23 and November 22). That pattern would make it November 21 this year, but I haven't seen a 2026 announcement, so treat it as a guess until their site says otherwise.

Snow without leaving the valley

Palm Springs never gets Christmas snow on the valley floor, but the Aerial Tramway lifts you from 2,643 feet to 8,516 feet in about ten minutes, and the top is typically 30 to 40 degrees cooler. Snow usually shows up from about November through April. The Tram's winter schedule runs October 5, 2026 to May 27, 2027, with the first car at 10 AM on weekdays and 8 AM on weekends and holidays, the last car up at 8 PM, and the last car down at 9:30 PM. Christmas Week, December 19 through January 3, uses the 8 AM start every day.

Patches of snow at the rail, the whole valley floor spread out underneath.

Holiday days can sell out, so buy tickets early, and dress for the top instead of the bottom: a real jacket, a hat, and shoes with grip. My Aerial Tram guide covers tickets, parking, and when to go.

What December weather means for your plans

December highs in Palm Springs average 69°F and the nights drop to about 43°F, so a 5:45 PM parade is a jacket event and the pool is a midday event (my best time to visit guide has the month-by-month table). Some restaurants and shops cut their hours around the holidays, and plenty of locals take the week between Christmas and New Year off, so book the dinner you care about before you land.

If you want a night out that isn't a parade, the weekly Thursday Village Fest runs year-round and the Palm Springs bar guide covers the rest.

Where I'd stay for the holidays

I can't see your dates from here, so check each calendar before you plan around anything above. Booking direct also skips Airbnb's 20% guest service fee, and the book direct page shows the math.

Indio: Terra Luz and The Cozy Cactus

Both sit inside Indian Palms Country Club, which puts you near the tamale festival and the Magic of Lights grounds, with Palm Springs about 30 to 40 minutes west. Terra Luz is a 3BR/2BA with a saltwater pool, complimentary spa heating, and optional pool heating for $75 a night (2-night minimum) or $400 a week with 24 hours' notice, which is worth knowing when the nights hit the low 40s. It's dog-friendly with approval.

The Terra Luz backyard, string lights already up.

The Cozy Cactus is a 3BR/2BA built for families, with a private hot tub, a putting green, three community pools through Indian Palms, and a baby and toddler setup already in the house. It doesn't accept pets. A hot tub under string lights is a fair answer to a 43-degree evening.

The Cozy Cactus hot tub at dusk, which is most of the argument for an Indio base in December.

Palm Springs: The Sundune

The Sundune is a second-floor 2BR/2BA condo with three king beds and a balcony facing the pool. Downtown Palm Springs is about 10 minutes away by car, not a walk, so for the parade you drive in and park off Palm Canyon. It's dog-friendly with approval, and the HOA sets a 4-night minimum stay, so it suits a long holiday weekend better than a single parade night.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the Palm Springs Festival of Lights Parade in 2026?

Saturday, December 5, 2026, starting at 5:45 PM on Palm Canyon Drive in downtown Palm Springs. It's free, and the city's holiday tree lighting happens the night before, on Friday, December 4.

When is the Indio Tamale Festival in 2026?

The 34th festival runs Saturday and Sunday, December 5 and 6, 2026, in downtown Indio. The City of Indio lists admission as free, and published hours vary by source, so check the festival's own site the week of.

Does it snow in Palm Springs at Christmas?

Not on the valley floor, where December highs average about 69°F. There is usually snow at the top of the Palm Springs Aerial Tramway, which sits at 8,516 feet and runs 30 to 40 degrees cooler than the desert.

Where can I see Christmas lights in the Coachella Valley?

The three best-known options are Magic of Lights, a drive-through show at the Empire Polo Club in Indio, WildLights at The Living Desert in Palm Desert, and Candy Cane Lane, a free residential street in Cathedral City. Their 2026 schedules were not published as of October 14, so check each organizer.

Is it warm enough to use the pool at Christmas?

Highs around 69°F make a heated pool pleasant at midday, but nights drop to about 43°F, so plan on heat or a hot tub. Terra Luz offers pool heating for $75 a night with a 2-night minimum and includes spa heating, and The Cozy Cactus has a private hot tub.
