Pioneertown Day Trip from Indio: What to Know Before You Go

By Eann Tuan · July 23, 2026 · 7 min read

Pioneertown doesn't look like a place that should exist. A dirt-street Old West town sitting in the high desert north of Joshua Tree, built in 1946 by a group of investors that included Roy Rogers and Dale Evans, meant to double as a working movie set and an actual town people could live in. More than fifty westerns got filmed on Mane Street through the 1950s. The town charter still forbids paving it, so you walk it the way the crews did.

It's a full but doable day trip from Indio, or a half-day if you're staying in Palm Springs. Here's what's there, what it costs, and how to time it.

The Mane Street sign. Pioneertown has looked roughly like this since the 1940s.

Getting There

From the Indio properties, plan on roughly an hour: I-10 west to Highway 62 north through Yucca Valley, then a left onto Pioneertown Road for the last few miles. From Palm Springs it's shorter, closer to 40-45 minutes on the same route. There's no shortcut through Joshua Tree National Park itself, the road runs around the park's north side.

Free parking is available at Pappy & Harriet's and along the east side of Mane Street. During ticketed shows, parking directly on Pioneertown Road gets restricted, so arrive early if there's a concert that night.

Mane Street Is Free

This is the part people don't expect: there's no entrance fee. Pioneertown isn't a state park or a paid attraction, it's an unincorporated community with a historic movie-set main street you're free to walk. The Mane Street Historic District is on the National Register of Historic Places, and most of the false-front buildings from the 1940s are still standing, including the Pioneertown Post Office, reportedly the most photographed post office in the country.

Shops along the street run mid-day to sundown, mostly on weekends, and several close Tuesday and Wednesday along with the restaurant. If you're coming on a weekday, call ahead or plan around Pappy & Harriet's, which stays open more consistently.

The drive up Highway 62 runs along this kind of high-desert terrain the whole way.

Pappy & Harriet's

The reason most people make the drive. Pappy & Harriet's started as a taco stand for movie crews and turned into one of the more unlikely music venues in the country, Robert Plant and Paul McCartney have both played unannounced sets there. It's open daily, functions as a straightforward BBQ restaurant during the day, and turns into a ticketed show venue at night depending on the calendar.

If there's a show you want to see, buy tickets ahead through their site. It's general admission, standing room only, and every attendee needs a ticket regardless of age. If you're just there for dinner, no ticket needed, but Friday and Saturday nights get busy and reservations help.

How Much Time to Budget

Walking Mane Street and popping into the shops takes an hour to two hours depending on how many stops you make. Add dinner at Pappy & Harriet's and you're looking at a half-day trip. Add a show and it becomes an evening: drive up in the late afternoon, eat, catch the set, drive back after.

Because the return drive is close to an hour, plan for the roads at night. They're unlit two-lane highway most of the way, straightforward but not something to rush.

Terrain like this fills most of the drive between Indio and the high desert.

Pair It With Joshua Tree

Pioneertown sits close enough to Joshua Tree National Park that a lot of visitors combine the two. The park's north and west entrances off Highway 62 are a short detour from the Pioneertown turnoff, so if you're already making the drive, it's worth checking our Joshua Tree day trip guide for entrance fees and timing before you commit to a single long day covering both.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there an entrance fee for Pioneertown?

No. Mane Street is a public street in an unincorporated community, not a paid attraction. You only pay for food, drinks, shopping, or tickets to a show at Pappy & Harriet's.

How far is Pioneertown from Indio?

About an hour by car via I-10 to Highway 62 north, then Pioneertown Road. From Palm Springs it's closer to 40-45 minutes on the same route.

Do I need a reservation for Pappy & Harriet's?

Not for a regular dinner, though weekend nights get busy and a reservation helps. For a ticketed show, yes, everyone attending needs a ticket bought in advance through their site.

Can I combine Pioneertown with a Joshua Tree day trip?

Yes. The park's north and west entrances off Highway 62 are a short detour from Pioneertown Road, so many visitors do both in one long day. See our Joshua Tree day trip guide for entrance details.

What days are Pioneertown shops closed?

Most shops and the restaurant at Pappy & Harriet's are closed Tuesdays and Wednesdays. Weekends are when Mane Street is fully open and most active.
