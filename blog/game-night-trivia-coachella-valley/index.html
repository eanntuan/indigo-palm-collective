7 Game Night and Trivia Spots in the Coachella Valley

By Eann Tuan · July 21, 2026 · 9 min read

By July the math in the desert is simple: mornings and evenings belong to you, and the middle of the day belongs to the sun. So the fun migrates indoors and after dark, which is exactly when the valley's game scene comes alive. Trivia teams filling up back rooms, casino bingo halls buzzing, a table of people painting tiny orcs in Palm Desert.

I moved out here from Culver City and assumed the desert shut down at night. It doesn't. There's a whole rotation of weekly game nights that most visitors never find because they're too busy chasing the same three midcentury photo spots. This is the honest list, organized by city, of where I'd send a guest looking for something to do on a Tuesday.

One caveat before you build a night around any of this: bar and lounge schedules shift constantly. Everything below is accurate as of 2026, but call ahead or check the venue's socials before you drive over, because trivia nights get moved and cancelled more than any restaurant reservation ever would.

Palm Springs

Palm Springs is where the trivia scene is thickest, and Tuesday is the night. You could genuinely bar-hop trivia across three venues in a single evening if you were feeling ambitious and a little foolish.

1. Quadz Video Bar

Tucked into the Arenas District, Quadz Video Bar runs Trivia Tuesdays and doubles as a karaoke spot the rest of the week, so the energy is loud and forgiving. The one I'd steer you toward is the Tiki-themed trivia night on Thursdays hosted by a local drag queen, which is more of a full evening than a quiz. It leans campy and it's better for it.

2. Plan B Live Entertainment & Cocktails

Plan B puts trivia on Tuesday evenings alongside its regular live entertainment calendar, so the room already has momentum by the time the first question drops. It's a good landing spot if you want cocktails and a show baked into the same night rather than a quiet quiz.

3. The Local on 50th

The Local on 50th runs Trivia Night on Tuesdays starting around 7pm, and it's the most neighborhood-bar version of the three: unfussy, regulars who take it a little too seriously, the kind of place you settle into. If your Tuesday doesn't line up, The Social Cafe & Lounge does trivia on the third Tuesday of every month, which is worth knowing if you're in town for a longer stretch and want to catch it.

Tuesday is trivia night across Palm Springs. Pick a bar, bring three friends who each know one useless category well.

Rather stay in? Honestly, some of the best game nights happen back at the rental after a long pool day, when nobody wants to put shoes back on. The Cozy Cactus in Indio has a dedicated game room with foosball, which turns a group of tired adults into a tournament bracket within about ten minutes. Terra Luz down the road has a long kitchen table and a lantern-strung patio that's made for cards once the heat lets go for the evening. Cathedral City and the casino bingo

If your idea of a game night is a room full of daubers and a shot at free slot play, the Agua Caliente casinos are the move, and they book-end the week nicely across two locations.

4. Agua Caliente Casinos (Palm Springs and Cathedral City)

The Agua Caliente Palm Springs location runs Hot Spot Bingo on Mondays in the lounge, hosted by comedy duo Deven Green and Handsome Ned. You play on your phone rather than a paper card, and prizes include free slot play, so it's less church-hall bingo and more late-night variety show. Over in Cathedral City, the sister casino does bingo on Wednesdays hosted by Eagle 106.9, with prizes running from gift cards up to concert tickets, plus sports-bar trivia the same night from 7 to 9pm. Both rooms are fully air conditioned, which in July counts as an amenity.

Desert Hot Springs

5. Playoffs Sports Lounge

Playoffs Sports Lounge got a 2024 Bar Rescue makeover, and the reboot stuck. Tuesday is trivia with prizes and drink specials, and Rainbow Wednesdays pair drag with bingo for a rowdier midweek option. Even on a night with nothing scheduled there's plenty to play, since the place is loaded with pool tables, darts, and shuffleboard. It's the spot I'd point a group to when the plan is loose and you just want somewhere to land.

Palm Desert

Palm Desert is where the game night scene gets genuinely nerdy, in the best sense. This is the end of the valley for people who'd rather roll dice or debate anime lore than answer pub-quiz questions about state capitals. It's also the closest cluster to our Indio properties, roughly 20 minutes from Indio.

6. Murasaki Kakurega Manga Lounge

Murasaki Kakurega is a manga reading lounge first, but at night it runs anime trivia that goes deep, think Naruto and Boruto lore rather than surface-level stuff, so bring a friend who actually finished the series. Beyond trivia it hosts card games including Pokemon, Magic: The Gathering, and the One Piece Card Game, which makes it a soft landing whether you're a competitive player or just there for the vibe and a drink.

The valley's game nights lean into low-lit, themed rooms once the sun's down. Half the fun is the setting.

7. The Dragon's Den

The Dragon's Den runs the valley's longest-running weekly tabletop event, every Thursday, hosted by mother-son duo Mindy and Jake Conant. On any given night you'll find miniature painting, Dungeons and Dragons campaigns, and Warhammer being played with the kind of focus you don't see much in a vacation town. If you travel with your own dice bag, this is your people. If you don't, it's still a fun window into a community that clearly loves what it does.

One more tip: follow the roving hosts

A chunk of the valley's best trivia isn't tied to a single bar, it follows the host. As of 2026, a trivia MC named Nate Fields runs Tuesday nights at rotating venues with categories that swing from Star Wars to earthquakes, and the prizes have included Acrisure Arena concert tickets. Worth a quick search for his current lineup before you commit to a specific bar, since the good hosts pull the good crowds.

The through-line here is that summer in the desert doesn't mean you're stuck. It just moves the fun to the edges of the day and indoors. Play a round of trivia, lose at bingo, paint an orc, then come back and finish the night on the patio.
