6 Best Public Golf Courses Near Palm Springs & Indio

By Eann Tuan · September 28, 2026 · 10 min read

Terra Luz and The Cozy Cactus both sit inside Indian Palms Country Club, which means our guests are usually thirty seconds from a tee box whether they came here to golf or not. That's not a coincidence I planned. It's just how Indio works: this whole stretch of the valley was built around fairways long before it was built around Airbnb.

The Coachella Valley has more public and resort golf than any desert this size has a right to, over a hundred courses between Palm Springs and La Quinta. Most of it isn't member-only. Below are six that welcome outside play, what each one costs to walk onto, and which one is the one our guests can reach without starting the car.

1. Indian Palms Country Club, Indio

48-630 Monroe Street, Indio. Twenty-seven holes split into three nines, the Mountain, Royal, and Indian, each a par 35 or 36 and none of them especially long, which is the point: this is a course that rewards accuracy over distance. Water comes into play on more than half the holes, and hundreds of mature trees line the fairways, unusual for a desert course. The signature hole is the Mountain Nine's 5th, a short par-4 with an island green.

It's open to public play every day, no membership required, and it's a piece of real Coachella Valley history: the original nine, built on what was the Cochran-Odlum Ranch, was the first public golf course in the valley. Check current tee times and rates on GolfNow before you go, pricing moves with season like every course on this list.

This is the one our guests don't have to drive to. Terra Luz and The Cozy Cactus both sit inside the Indian Palms community, close enough to walk your clubs over.

Terra Luz's patio. The course is a shorter walk from here than the rental car is from long-term parking.

2. Desert Willow Golf Resort, Palm Desert

38-995 Desert Willow Drive, Palm Desert. A 36-hole public facility with two distinct courses: Firecliff, 7,056 yards and par 72, built around more than a hundred bunkers and water on nearly every hole, and Mountain View, a gentler 6,913-yard round with wider fairways for a more forgiving day. Both are city-owned and open to anyone, and Firecliff has been on magazine covers for its conditioning.

This is the pick if you want a genuine championship test in the morning and an easier warm-down round in the afternoon without leaving the parking lot. Golf Digest has rated it among the best public courses in the country more than once.

3. SilverRock Resort, La Quinta

79-179 Ahmanson Ln, La Quinta. The city of La Quinta's own municipal course, an Arnold Palmer design at 7,239 yards, the longest public course in the valley, sitting right against the base of the Santa Rosa Mountains. It hosted the PGA Tour's American Express tournament (formerly the Bob Hope Classic) from 2008 to 2011, and it's open to the public every day, no resort stay required.

The back nine went through a turf renovation earlier this year and reopened in late August, so the greens should be in some of the best shape they've been in a while if you're booking now.

4. Indian Wells Golf Resort

Indian Wells. Another 36-hole public facility, the Players Course and the Celebrity Course, both par 72 and both ranked among the top municipal courses in the country by Golfweek. The Celebrity Course has hosted the PGA Tour's Skins Game twice, and the clubhouse runs a full restaurant if you want to make a day of it rather than just a round.

About 15 minutes from Terra Luz and The Cozy Cactus, and one of the better-conditioned public options if Indian Palms and Desert Willow are booked up.

5. Tahquitz Creek Golf Resort, Palm Springs

1885 Golf Club Drive, Palm Springs. A city-owned public facility with two courses: the Resort Course, a desert-links layout with water features and mountain views, and the Legend Course, a tree-lined traditional layout that's been a local favorite for decades. This is the value pick on the list, municipal pricing without a municipal-course feel.

If you're staying at The Sundune in Palm Springs, this is the closest course on this list to you, and one of the easier ones to book same-week.

6. PGA West, La Quinta

La Quinta. The name most golfers already know. Three of PGA West's courses, the Pete Dye Stadium Course, the Greg Norman Course, and the Jack Nicklaus Tournament Course, are open to outside play through La Quinta Resort & Club, not just members. The Stadium Course is the famous one, home to some of the most photographed and most punishing holes in televised golf, and it plays to it: green fees run roughly $146 to $264 depending on season and time of day, well above everything else on this list.

Book this one as the splurge round of the trip, not the daily one. Resort guests staying at La Quinta Resort get earlier booking windows and better rates than outside play, so if PGA West is the whole reason for the trip, that's worth knowing before you book your stay elsewhere.

Planning a Golf Trip to the Coachella Valley

Book tee times as far ahead as you can for anything from December through April, the valley's cooler months are also its busiest golf months, and the good weekend slots at Desert Willow, Indian Wells, and PGA West go fast. Summer is the opposite problem: cheap twilight rates and nearly empty courses, if you can handle the heat starting before 9am.

If Indian Palms is your course, you don't need a hotel at all. Book Terra Luz or The Cozy Cactus direct at indigopalm.co, skip Airbnb's 20% guest service fee, and walk to your tee time instead of driving to it. For the rest of the valley's courses, both properties put you within 20 minutes of everything on this list.

Frequently Asked Questions

What golf course is closest to Indio vacation rentals?

Indian Palms Country Club is inside the Indian Palms community in Indio, the same neighborhood as our Terra Luz and Cozy Cactus rentals. It's a 27-hole public course, no membership required, and guests staying there can walk to the first tee.

Can the public play PGA West in La Quinta?

Yes, three courses, the Stadium Course, the Greg Norman Course, and the Jack Nicklaus Tournament Course, are open to outside play through La Quinta Resort & Club. Resort guests get earlier booking windows and lower rates than the general public, and green fees on the Stadium Course run roughly $146 to $264 depending on season.

What's the most affordable public golf course in the Coachella Valley?

Tahquitz Creek Golf Resort in Palm Springs and SilverRock Resort in La Quinta are both municipally owned and priced accordingly, well below resort courses like PGA West. Twilight rates in summer bring prices down further across almost every course in the valley.

When is the best time to golf in the Coachella Valley?

December through April brings the best weather and the highest demand, book tee times well ahead for that stretch. Summer months are brutally hot by midday but bring the cheapest rates and emptiest courses if you tee off early.
