Box Canyon Hike Near Indio: Sheep Hole Oasis Guide

By Eann Tuan · October 19, 2026 · 7 min read

The Box Canyon hike near Indio is a walk to Sheep Hole Oasis, a small palm oasis in the Mecca Hills Wilderness about 15 miles southeast of town. The shorter route is roughly 2.9 miles round trip, and a longer loop that adds Hidden Springs runs about 5.75 miles. It is free according to the BLM, there is no marked trail, and the best season is October through April, starting early.

Quick facts

  • Where: Mecca Hills Wilderness (BLM), about 15 miles southeast of Indio, reached from Box Canyon Road
  • Distance: about 2.9 miles round trip to Sheep Hole Oasis (AllTrails, moderate), or 5.75 miles with 800+ feet of gain on the longer Grottos route (strenuous)
  • Cost: the BLM page lists no fee and no permit information
  • Best season: October through April, early morning starts
  • Cell service: little to none, so download offline maps first
  • Skip it: summer afternoons, and any day with rain in the forecast

What Sheep Hole Oasis and Hidden Springs Look Like

The Bureau of Land Management manages the 26,242-acre Mecca Hills Wilderness and calls Sheep Hole Oasis and Hidden Springs Canyon reliable water sources in the small southern section, split off by the Box Canyon Road corridor. The terrain is a badlands maze of narrow canyons shaped by the San Andreas Fault, with rock layers more than 600 million years old.

Palm groves in the desert photograph better than they hike, so set expectations accordingly. Hiker accounts describe Sheep Hole as a small cluster of fan palms rather than a full grove, which makes the walk through the washes the real draw and the palms the reward at the end.

Mecca Hills badlands with the Salton Sea in the distance. Photo: BLM California / Wikimedia Commons, public domain.

Driving to the Box Canyon Trailhead From Indio

The BLM says you can reach the wilderness from the east on Highway 195 (Box Canyon Road) via Interstate 10, or from the south at Mecca off Highway 111. Box Canyon Road is a paved county road that cuts through the hills, with no services along the way, so fill your tank in Indio.

Friends of the Desert Mountains meets its guided hikes on Box Canyon Road in Mecca, then walks a short way to the trailhead at 33.589100, -115.930060. Enter those coordinates in an offline map before you leave, and confirm road status with the BLM at 760-833-7100, since Box Canyon Road and Painted Canyon Road can close seasonally for weather.

Box Canyon Road passing the Mecca Hills rock formations. Photo: DylanMoz49 / Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA 4.0.

Do You Need 4WD or High Clearance?

The BLM describes Painted Canyon Road, the route to the Ladder Canyon trailheads, as four-wheel-drive access, so skip it in a low car. For Box Canyon Road itself, a regular vehicle handles the pavement, and anything beyond it depends on that week's conditions, which is a question for the BLM rather than a forum thread.

Sheep Hole Oasis Trail Length and Difficulty

The BLM states plainly that the area has no designated trails. You follow a worn route through washes and over ridges, and mileage varies by source.

AllTrails lists Sheep Hole Oasis via the Hidden Spring Trail at 2.9 miles with 538 feet of gain, rated moderate. The longer Grottos route that links both oases is strenuous at 5.75 miles with more than 800 feet of gain, according to Friends of the Desert Mountains, who lead it as a guided hike. It starts on a ridge with Salton Sea views, drops down a steep, uneven path into a sandy wash, and returns past the palms at Hidden Springs.

Hikers in a Mecca Hills wash, the kind of terrain the oasis routes follow. Photo: BLM California / Wikimedia Commons, public domain.

The grotto at the end of that route collapsed years ago, so the hike now finishes at the canyon where its entrance used to be. Older trip reports promising a cave describe a place that no longer exists.

Narrow canyon walls in the Mecca Hills. Photo: BLM California / Wikimedia Commons, public domain.

Stay in Indio, Hike Before Breakfast

Start time matters more than fitness out here. Guests at Terra Luz or The Cozy Cactus, both in Indio and walking distance to the Empire Polo Club, can head out at sunrise and be back at the pool before the afternoon heat builds. Staying in Palm Springs at The Sundune means a longer drive, since the BLM describes the Mecca Hills as about an hour southeast of Palm Springs, so leave earlier.

Hiking Safety: Heat, Flash Floods, and No Cell Service

The BLM's own advice is short and worth following: carry at least one liter of water per person plus electrolytes, start early because temperatures rise fast by early afternoon, hike with a buddy, and tell someone your expected departure and return times. Friends of the Desert Mountains asks guided hikers to bring 3 liters, closed-toed shoes, a hat, and sunscreen, which is a sensible number for the longer route.

Flash floods are the other hazard. The National Park Service warns in nearby Joshua Tree that flash floods can hit dry canyons and washes, can happen with sunny skies overhead, and cannot be outrun. Check the forecast before you leave, watch for cloud buildup, and skip the hike if rain is possible anywhere upstream.

A blooming palo verde in a Mecca Hills wash. Photo: BLM California / Wikimedia Commons, public domain.

Download your map and the trail track before you leave. A paper backup costs nothing and weighs less than a bottle of water.

Best Time to Hike Sheep Hole Oasis (and What to Skip in Summer)

AllTrails lists October through April as the best window, which fits how the Coachella Valley hikes in general: crisp mornings, clear air, and the Salton Sea glinting below the ridge. From roughly June through September, skip it. There is almost no shade and cell service is minimal, and the BLM says temperatures climb quickly by early afternoon.

For a summer-friendly palm canyon, the shaded trails in our Palm Springs hiking guide are a better bet.

What to Pack for the Box Canyon Hike

  • 3 liters of water per person for the longer route, plus electrolytes
  • Closed-toed shoes with grip, and hiking poles if your knees like them
  • Hat, sunscreen, and snacks
  • Offline map, a charged phone, and a backup battery
  • A flashlight, in case you push into darker canyon sections
  • A full gas tank, since Box Canyon Road has no services

Frequently Asked Questions

How far is the Sheep Hole Oasis hike from Indio?

The BLM places Mecca Hills Wilderness about 15 miles southeast of Indio. The shorter Sheep Hole route is about 2.9 miles round trip per AllTrails.

Do you need a permit or pay a fee to hike Box Canyon?

The BLM's Mecca Hills page lists no fee and no permit information. Rules can change, so confirm with the BLM Palm Springs South Coast Field Office at 760-833-7100 before you go.

Do you need 4WD to reach the Sheep Hole Oasis trailhead?

Box Canyon Road is paved, but the BLM describes Painted Canyon Road as four-wheel-drive access, and both roads can close for weather. Check current conditions with the BLM before driving out.

Is the Sheep Hole Oasis hike safe in summer?

Skip it from roughly June through September. Heat rises fast, there is little shade, and cell service is minimal. October through April is the usual window.

Is there cell service on the trail?

The BLM says very little to none. Download offline maps and share your plan with someone before you leave.
