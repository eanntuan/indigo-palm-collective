Terra Luz: Latin Desert Vacation Rental near Coachella

Indio, California | Earth & Light

Cocooned. Vibrant. Distinctly somewhere.

Old Havana warmth. One Frida-blue pool.

Step through the door at Terra Luz and the pace changes. The terracotta courtyard, the Kahlo blue pool catching afternoon light, the sound of nothing but desert and whoever's in the kitchen. It feels like a place that's been lived in, not just decorated.

Cuban roasted coffee beans on the counter, Mexican dominos on the shelf, patterned robes in the closets. The Kitchen Terrace has a pass-through window that slides open toward the pool, so morning coffee becomes poolside conversation without anyone actually having to move. The whole thing was put together by Dawn Asher, and it shows, rattan and terracotta and zero generic anything.

Three bedrooms, two bathrooms, sleeps up to eight, primary suite facing the pool. The kitchen is stocked properly, gas stove and everything you need if you actually want to cook. Spa heating is on us and always running, and pool heating runs $75 a night with a two-night minimum if you want it warm.

Why This House

Most vacation rentals look the same. We got tired of staying in them so we stopped building them. Terra Luz was designed by Dawn Asher of The Olive Jar to feel like it has a past, like someone collected these things over years instead of clicking a wishlist. Old Havana character through a desert lens, which sounds like marketing copy until you're actually standing in it.

Around 4-5pm the pool turns that specific shade of blue that makes you put down your phone. The pass-through kitchen window lets someone hand you a coffee without leaving the pool deck. Business & Pleasure pillows on the patio, West Elm furniture that actually holds up in desert heat. It's the details, and yes I know everyone says that.

Location

Terra Luz is inside Indian Palms Country Club, a gated neighborhood in Indio with pay-to-play golf, tennis courts, and community pools. Empire Polo Club is walking distance, no shuttle required. During Coachella and Stagecoach that's the difference between walking out when you want and waiting 40 minutes for an Uber. Indian Wells Tennis Garden is twenty minutes east, ideal for BNP Paribas guests who want a private home over a tournament hotel. Palm Springs is thirty minutes west, Joshua Tree forty-five minutes northeast, downtown Indio five minutes in any direction.

Who Stays Here

Friend groups who want a private pool and somewhere with actual personality, families who need a real kitchen and a real outdoor room, and people who've been burned enough times by generic rentals that they read the reviews first. Festival crowds book it because being walking distance from the polo grounds on your own schedule beats any shuttle, full stop. The pool is exactly as saturated in person as it looks in photos, I get asked that a lot.

Frequently Asked Questions

The things people actually ask.

Is Terra Luz pet-friendly? +

Yes, up to 2 dogs, $150 pet fee. Fenced backyard, dog bowl and treats included. Pets stay off the furniture and can't be left alone in the house. Email indigopalmco@gmail.com before booking to confirm your pup is good to go.

How far is Terra Luz from Coachella and Stagecoach? +

Walking distance to Empire Polo Club. One of the closest vacation rentals to the festival grounds in the Coachella Valley, and the walk means no shuttles, no surge pricing at midnight.

What makes Terra Luz unique? +

Design-wise: Dawn Asher of The Olive Jar did the whole thing, and it's Latin/Cuban-inspired in a specific, deliberate way, not generic boho. Kahlo blue saltwater pool, terracotta courtyard, things that feel like they were collected over years. As far as I know, it's the only vacation rental in the Coachella Valley with an actual Old Havana design identity.

How many guests does Terra Luz sleep? +

Up to 8 guests in 3 bedrooms, on three king beds plus a twin trundle. Full kitchen, living and dining areas, private saltwater pool with a tanning ledge and heated spa, and a covered outdoor patio. It's a real house, not a cramped rental.

What is the minimum stay at Terra Luz? +

2 nights minimum. Check-in is 4pm, checkout is 10am. Early check-in or late checkout can sometimes happen if the calendar is clear, just ask ahead of time.

How much do I save booking directly? +

Airbnb tacks a 20% guest service fee onto your subtotal and VRBO isn't far behind. Booking direct means you pay what you see, and you have my number if anything comes up.
