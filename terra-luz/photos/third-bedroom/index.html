CALI ROOM

Third Bedroom

Quiet corner. Desert light.

The Cali Room is the one guests gravitate toward when they want a little separation from the rest of the house. It sits in its own corner, catches morning light through the window, and has the kind of warm, settled tone that makes you want to sleep in. The queen bed is dressed simply, with linens chosen for feel over flash, and the palette runs through sand, rust, and the muted golds you find in late-afternoon desert sun.

The decor here leans into California desert without shouting it. Think woven textures, low-profile furniture, and a few carefully chosen pieces that give the room personality without clutter. Nothing on the walls is filler. The natural light does most of the work, and there is enough of it at the right hours to make the whole room shift from cool in the morning to amber by late afternoon.

It is a good room for anyone who wants to wake up slowly. Close the door, pull the blinds halfway, and the Cali Room becomes as quiet and unhurried as the desert itself.
