TERRA LUZ

Primary Bedroom

The room I would most want to sleep in.

Custom tile from LA Tile, installed by Orlin over two days. Floor decisions either date a room fast or define it for years. This one defines it.

Sage green walls, a ribbed wood headboard, pendant lamps Dawn approved after the fourth round of options. A rattan circle mirror and wall sconce at exactly the right height behind the bed. The woven lounge chair in the corner looks three times what it cost. (Facebook Marketplace. Worth knowing.) Coffee bar with a Keurig in warm stone against the wall, and above it, large-format art that fills the space in a way a standard print never would.

On the far wall, a fireplace niche with accent tile reads as its own design moment. The cane daybed in the sitting area pulls a trundle underneath, so the suite sleeps three when the group needs it without giving anything up. We tried canopy drapes before the shoot. The rods sat too close to the bed frame to work structurally, so we pulled them. Nothing was lost.
