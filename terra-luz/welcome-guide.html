Getting here

We live inside Indian Palms Country Club, a gated community, and check-in is at 4 pm. Type "Indian Palms Country Club" into your GPS and it takes you to the Monroe St gate.

Open directions to 49768 Pacino St

Monroe gate is the main gate. Give the guard your name and our address, or pick up a paper guest pass, or use the Conekt app on the iPod Touch once you are inside to use the "transponder only" lane.

The Ave 48, Jackson and Ave 50 gates need mobile phone credentials (including the Conekt app on the iPod Touch) or a license plate registered with us. If more than one driver is coming, send us names and plates, one per vehicle. The club monitors every vehicle, so allow extra time.

Check in

1. Your code. We send a 4 digit access code at least 24 hours before arrival, and it only works from check-in until check-out.

2. The smart lock. Type the code and the lock should unlock on its own. If it gives you trouble, message us.

3. Parking. The garage fits 2 cars and the driveway fits 2 more. Day guests can park on the street from 7 am to midnight, and security patrol tickets and tows overnight street parking.

Noise curfew starts at 10 pm. Neighbors report and the city can issue hefty citations for excessive noise, Bluetooth speakers on the patio included.

Coffee

Cafe Bustelo grounds and Peet's whole beans are waiting, and the mugs are handmade by local artist Leigh Dierck. Creamer is tricky in the desert, but L&G Desert Store down the road carries local dairy.

Keurig. Pods are in the primary bedroom coffee cabinet.

French press. The Fellow press works well with the Peet's beans, and the grinder is in the cabinet below.

Pour over. Kettle, dripper and filters are set up and ready.

Pardon the dust

Desert wind is part of the experience, so a little dust on arrival is normal. We left colored towels and a leaf blower for a quick refresh before you settle in.

Pool and spa

The backyard was built to keep you outside: pool, spa, string lights and desert air.

Spa heat and lights

Spa heat is complimentary and takes about an hour to reach 103. The Spa button on the wall panel runs the jets for one hour, and the Lights button flips the underwater lights. Please turn the spa and jets off when you are done.

Pool heat

Pool heat and spa heat cannot run at the same time. If you paid for pool heating, the spa is off while the pool heats, and the other way around.

Umbrella and floaties

Open umbrellas do not survive desert wind, so close it and bring it in when you are done. Store the floaties in the garage, where the electric pump is too, because direct sun breaks them down fast.

Pool rules

No diving. The pool is only 5 feet deep.

No glass. Plastic dishware and cups are in the kitchen.

No water balloons and no food in the pool. Eat at the patio table and do not leave food out overnight, because it attracts pests.

BBQ

The built-in gas BBQ is ready to use. Turn on the gas line to the left of the tanning ledge and turn it off when you finish. Utensils are in the kitchen drawer and plastic dinnerware is in the cabinet left of the dishwasher.

Community amenities

You have access to three community heated pools, spas, the fitness center, and the Lifestyle Center tennis and pickleball courts. Courts usually need no reservation, and equipment rents with an ID. Pool hours are 8 am to 10 pm.

Fitness center and main pool

Message us the names of all guests and we will submit a form for a temporary card, and only 4 guests can enter at a time. The main pool asks for a $10 deposit on a temporary membership card at the gym front desk, refunded when you return it.
