Why It Matters Beyond the Fee

Four honest reasons to book direct

You talk to the actual host

When you book through Airbnb, every message routes through their platform, where it can sit for hours and gets screened against their policies before it even reaches me. Book direct and you have my real contact info, a faster reply, and a conversation that actually goes somewhere.

Flexible check-in and checkout conversations are possible

Airbnb sets the times, and that's that. When you book direct, early check-in or a slightly late checkout is something we can actually talk about depending on the schedule. I can't promise yes every time, but I can have the conversation with you, which is more than the platform allows.

Better prep on my end

When a booking comes in directly, I know more about who's staying before they arrive. That means I can prep the right things: leave the pack-n-play out if you're coming with an infant, have the fridge stocked if you mention an early arrival, or set the pool heat ahead of time. Airbnb strips a lot of that context out.

The review system works differently

Airbnb's review process is managed by Airbnb, which means disputes, removals, and pressure to leave five stars in both directions. Direct bookings don't have that dynamic. If something's wrong, we just talk about it, fix it if we can, and move on. It's a cleaner relationship for everyone.
