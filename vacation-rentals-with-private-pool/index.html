Why a Private Pool

What changes when the pool is actually yours

No pool hours

A hotel pool closes at 5pm. Ours don't. The best desert pool hour is 9pm on a warm April night, and you shouldn't need permission for that.

The 3pm recovery window

Festival guests figured this out years ago: leave at 3pm, pool for 90 minutes, eat something real, back for the evening headliner. This only works if your rental is close and the pool is private.

Worth more than square footage

A private pool in the Coachella Valley adds more to the experience per dollar than any extra bedroom. Desert nights in April drop to the low 60s. The pool gets used.

Both properties have one

Cozy Cactus has a private hot tub plus community pool access. Terra Luz has a fully private saltwater pool. Different vibes, same desert logic.

FAQ

What people ask before booking

Which properties have a private pool (not shared)? +

Terra Luz has a fully private saltwater pool, so no other guests use it, there are no hours, and there's no reservation system. Cozy Cactus has a private hot tub on the property plus access to the Indian Palms Country Club community pool, which is shared with the neighborhood but never crowded in our experience. Both are in Indio.

Is the pool heated? +

At Terra Luz, pool heating is available for $75 per night, which is worth it if you're visiting outside peak summer months since April and October nights drop to the 50s, and an unheated pool is mostly decorative after 8pm. Cozy Cactus's hot tub is heated and included.

Which pool property is best for a festival group? +

Terra Luz for a group that wants the private pool as the centerpiece. Cozy Cactus for a group that wants both hot tub privacy and a larger community pool option nearby. Both are in Indian Palms, walking distance to the Empire Polo Club.

Can I bring my dog to a pool rental? +

Terra Luz welcomes up to 2 dogs with a $150 pet fee and has a fully fenced yard. Cozy Cactus is pet-free. Email indigopalmco@gmail.com before booking with your dog's details.
